Parsons is now hiring for a **Public Affairs Intern** to support the work of the **Hudson Tunnel Project Community Engagement Centers (CECs)** by helping connect community members with project information, resources, and engagement opportunities. This role is ideal for students who enjoy working with people, including schoolchildren, communicating creatively, and making a positive impact on their community. Interns will report directly to the Manager of Community Engagement Centers and will gain hands\-on experience in community engagement, public communication, event planning, and stakeholder relations, skills valuable across public policy, communications, transportation, education, and nonprofit sectors.

**What You'll Be Doing:**

* Support daily operations at both the New York and New Jersey CECs, greeting community members who visit, helping answer questions and addressing concerns related to the project.
* Assist in planning and facilitating community meetings and events (which may require some traveling beyond the CECs).
* Help gather community feedback and track questions, concerns, and suggestions using tracking mechanisms specific to the project.
* Collaborate with local organizations, schools, clubs, and community groups to support outreach efforts and drive traffic to the CECs.
* Create and distribute communication materials such as flyers, newsletters, social media content, and project updates in coordination with the Communications Department.
* Help coordinate educational workshops, site tours, and youth engagement activities that support the project.
* Serve as a friendly, approachable point of contact between the project team and community members.
* Contribute ideas for new engagement initiatives or campaigns to improve community participation.

**What You’ll Gain**

* Real\-world experience in community outreach and public engagement.
* Professional development in communication, event coordination, and stakeholder relations.
* Exposure to large\-scale infrastructure and transportation projects.
* Mentorship from experienced outreach and project professionals.
* A meaningful role supporting community voices and public involvement

**Applicants should be enrolled in one of the following majors:**

* Communications, Marketing, Public Relations
* Psychology, Sociology, Education
* Public Policy, Urban Studies, Political Science
* Transportation, Engineering, Environmental Studies
* STEAM majors interested in public engagement
