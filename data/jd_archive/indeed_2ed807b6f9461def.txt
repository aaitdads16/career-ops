Garrett Motion is seeking a motivated college intern to support our **Burst and Containment (B\&C)** project by leveraging AI and Machine Learning techniques. The role involves organizing, cleaning, and structuring historical B\&C data (both test and FEA) to enable predictive modeling and intelligent systems. The intern will help build a robust data pipeline and AI\-driven tools that improve estimation of design requirements, wheel weakening values, and facilitate continuous improvement through an autonomous AI agent for B\&C\-related queries.


**Key Responsibilities:**

* **Data Preparation \& Management:**


	+ Collect, organize, and clean historical B\&C test and FEA data.
	+ Design and implement structured data architecture for ML/AI applications.
	+ Ensure data integrity, consistency, and scalability.
* **Machine Learning \& AI Development:**


	+ Assist in developing ML models to predict CB wall thickness and wheel weakening values for B\&C test.
	+ Support integration of AI algorithms for QBS.
	+ Collaborate on creating an AI agent for B\&C\-related queries using existing data.
* **System Development \& Continuous Improvement:**


	+ Build a robust system for QBS leveraging AI insights.
	+ Identify gaps and propose enhancements in data and model performance.
	+ Document processes and maintain version control for datasets and models.
* **Collaboration \& Reporting:**


	+ Work closely with simulation engineers and project leads.
	+ Prepare progress reports and present findings to the team.

**Skills \& Competencies**

* **Technical Skills:**


	+ Python programming, data cleaning, ML libraries (Pandas, Scikit\-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch).
	+ Basic understanding of FEA concepts and mechanical systems.
	+ **Optional:** Exposure to **LS\-Dyna** , **ANSYS** , FEA tools, and structural mechanics.
* **Soft Skills:**


	+ Analytical thinking and problem\-solving.
	+ Ability to work independently and in a team.
	+ Strong communication and documentation skills.

**Learning Outcomes**

* Hands\-on experience in applying AI/ML to mechanical engineering problems.
* Understanding of data engineering workflows for simulation and test data.
* Exposure to predictive modeling for structural analysis and FEA (ANSYS/LS\-Dyna).
* Opportunity to work on cutting\-edge AI applications in engineering and FEA Simulation.
