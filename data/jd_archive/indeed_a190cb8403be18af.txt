**OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENT:**
The Fleet Technologies Group leads a portfolio of innovative projects focused on enhancing operations, customer communications, and depot operations.

Automatic camera enforcement (ACE) seeks to use cutting edge camera, license plate recognition, and AI/ML technology to identify violations of traffics laws such as bus lane, bus stop or double\-parking infractions. In this internship, you'll dive into real\-world systems—reviewing vendor compliance with requirements, testing their results –generally in office but occasionally out in the field, and shaping transit network descriptions. You’ll analyze, build scripts and dashboards and present data on operational and financial metrics, and collaborate with vendor AI models to uncover process improvements.
**RESPONSIBILITIES:**
The intern will, under professional guidance:

* Create charts and diagrams illustrating system functionality.

**PROJECTS:**
The ACE program comprises three core sub\-projects that interns will actively support:

* Requirements Compliance – oversee and inspect the equipment during installation and then later analyze operational data to ensure compliance with technical specifications.
* Operational Metrics – Aggregate these data, assist in generating performance reports, and support troubleshooting efforts as needed.
* Financial Reporting – Consolidate financial data from operational activities and contribute to the preparation of accurate financial reports.
