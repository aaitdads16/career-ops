#### **Position Overview:**



We are looking for an **Art Intern** who is passionate about honing their craft and growing their skills. By joining Gram Games, you'll develop your skills alongside some of the most talented minds in mobile gaming. Throughout this internship, you will gain deep industry knowledge and contribute directly to the production of our titles.



At Gram, our interns are integral members of the team. We're seeking someone driven, proactive, and unafraid to take initiative. We expect our interns to leave a lasting mark on both our studio and our games, and we look for teammates who embody flexibility, independence, and a restless curiosity to find creative answers.



At Gram we embrace a hybrid work environment. Within this role, you would work 2 days per week in our London office and the remaining days at home.

#### **What You'll Do:**


* **Create 2D Art Assets:** Produce high\-quality visuals for LiveOps events, including offers, pop\-ups, banners, and in\-game assets.

* **Collaborative Feedback:** Actively participate in art reviews, applying and providing constructive feedback to maintain high visual standards.
