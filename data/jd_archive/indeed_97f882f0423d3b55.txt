As a Production \& Service Excellence Analyst Intern, you will support global operations and supply chain teams in driving operational efficiency and data\-driven decision\-making. You will contribute to the development of KPI dashboards, data analysis, and process documentation to support strategic initiatives across service and production operations. This role offers hands\-on exposure to business intelligence tools, supply chain systems, and cross\-functional collaboration within a global manufacturing and IT environment.

#### **What you will do**

* Assist in developing and visualizing KPI dashboards using Power BI to support global service and production performance tracking
* Support the collection, consolidation, and analysis of data from multiple systems and data sources
* Evaluate and propose effective methods for structuring and managing data to improve reporting and operational insights
* Contribute to the creation of operational and technical documentation, including data acquisition and reporting processes
* Support ongoing operations and process improvement projects, gaining exposure to real\-world business and supply chain challenges
* Collaborate with global stakeholders across Supply Chain, Manufacturing Operations, Procurement, IT teams, and external consultants.

#### **What you bring**

* Undergraduate pursuing a degree in IT, Computer Science, Supply Chain, or a related discipline
* Interest or exposure to data warehousing, data mining, or big data concepts
* Familiarity with Power BI, Excel cubes, or other BI/reporting tools
* Exposure to Power Apps, Power Automate, and data connectors is an advantage
* Basic understanding of APIs and system integrations with third\-party applications or data sources
* Strong analytical, problem\-solving, and communication skills
* Proactive mindset with willingness to learn in a global, cross\-functional environment.
