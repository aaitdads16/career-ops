The Autonomous and Intelligent Systems Department works to enable effective and safe adoption of embodied AI solutions for the most critical challenges. Our technical team of experts, developers, and strategists are helping to realize the benefits autonomy will bring across various sectors including defense, healthcare, transportation, and law enforcement. We bring our unique and unbiased understanding of missions, the innovation landscape, and technology to focus in areas where industry is not incentivized or able to operate to bring insights and solutions to MITRE’s sponsors and partners.

**Roles \& Responsibilities:**

* Maintain knowledge of advances of autonomy and robotics capabilities
* Leverage technical expertise to evaluate and improve AI/Autonomy system performance
* Provide thought leadership centered around autonomy/AI technology adoption on robotics hardware and cloud\-based computing environments
* Evaluate autonomy or AI solutions and provide guidance to sponsors based on their requirements
* Work on topics in robotics\-relevant fields, such as computer vision, deep learning, machine learning, planning, human machine teaming, and multi\-robot systems
* Build capabilities in one of more growth area for Autonomous Systems (e.g., maritime autonomy, healthcare robotics, space robotics)
* Help department and division execute strategy around enabling high\-consequence applications of Autonomy, including necessary assurance, T\&E, and societal implications
