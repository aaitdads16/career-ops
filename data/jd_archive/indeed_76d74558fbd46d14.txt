**About the Team**
------------------



DoorDash is building the world's most reliable on\-demand logistics platform. As part of our global expansion, we are investing heavily in Brazil and growing our engineering presence in São Paulo. This is a unique opportunity to join one of Silicon Valley's fastest\-growing companies—while staying close to home.



We're looking for Software Engineers interns who are excited to solve real\-world problems at scale. You'll work on product teams or platform teams that power our three\-sided marketplace of **Consumers, Dashers, and Merchants**. From improving restaurant discovery to optimizing delivery logistics, you'll help shape the experience of millions of users.



You'll work alongside experienced engineers, product managers, and designers—many based in the U.S.—so fluency in English and strong collaboration skills are essential.


**About the Role**
------------------



At DoorDash, our product engineers implement and operate technological solutions to improve the experiences of our Consumers, Dashers and Merchants. From creating beautiful, user\-friendly flows to crafting scalable backend architectures, we strive to deliver reliant, performant technology that delights our customers.



We believe interns are essential to our mission of attracting and retaining top talent. During our Software Engineer internship, interns are completely immersed in their teams and will have the opportunity to develop, maintain, and ship products. As an intern, you will directly impact our business by collaborating with your team to solve real problems for our customers.



The internship program will be hybrid with at least 1 day a week required to be in\-person in our Sao Paulo office. You will be paired with a mentor to help you ramp up and get comfortable working at DoorDash!
