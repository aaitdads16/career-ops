The internship



Join our Global Talent \& Performance Management Team and play a relevant role in supporting our global initiatives aimed at building a winning people \& culture organisation. By joining our team, you'll have the opportunity to:


* Support key projects related to Performance Management in HEINEKEN (e.g. elevating our feedback culture, processes transformation, AI for talent, etc.)
* Collaborate on the development of materials and support resources that help build a high\-performance culture
* Assist with the facilitation of training sessions for the HEINEKEN People Function community worldwide
* Assist in change management and communication campaigns
* Support topics related to the broader talent and performance management agenda.


Your new team and workplace



Our team’s mission is to Brew the best talent pipelines and elevate our performance culture to create a winning future\-ready workforce. We can only reach this by focusing on Identifying winning talent, Building robust talent pipelines and Strengthening leadership effectiveness.



During your internship, you’ll have every opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as an organization, not just within your own team, but across departments. We trust and empower you to work with an entrepreneurial mindset. At HEINEKEN, we love bringing the outside world in and welcome fresh, new ideas with open arms. Of course, we’ll make sure you get the guidance that suits you.



The unique you!



We believe everyone has unique talents, which is why we’re looking for you! Energy, drive and the ambition to grow are what make the difference at HEINEKEN. In addition, you bring:


* Currently in HBO or WO education
* Fluent English language required
* Eager to learn and high learning agility
* Pro\-active, pragmatic with a hands\-on mentality, focused on quality deliverables
* Team worker with the ability to manage your own projects and juggle priorities
* Excellent communication skills, well\-versed at wrapping up complex ideas and projects in a concise way and ability to connect with diverse people and cultures
* Experienced user of MS Office (Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. It is a plus if you are familiar with working data analytics tools such as PowerBI)

Even if you feel you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply. An internship is the perfect opportunity to learn and grow!



Conditions of HEINEKEN
