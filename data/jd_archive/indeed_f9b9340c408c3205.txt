Morgan Stanley Investment Management (“MSIM”), together with its investment advisory affiliates, has more than 1,300 investment professionals around the world and $1\.9 trillion in assets under management or supervision as of December 31, 2025\. MSIM strives to provide strong long\-term investment performance, outstanding service and a comprehensive suite of investment management solutions to a diverse client base, which includes governments, institutions, corporations and individuals worldwide.


Morgan Stanley Real Assets (“MSRA”) is the global real assets investment management arm of Morgan Stanley. MSRA is comprised of four investment teams focused on private real estate equity, private infrastructure equity and private real assets credit strategies. With 20 offices in 13 countries throughout the U.S., Europe and Asia, regional teams of dedicated real assets professionals combine a unique global perspective with local presence and significant transaction execution expertise. As of December 31, 2025, MSRA manages $80 billion of investable capital worldwide.


**Brief Description – About the Internship**

During this internship, you will gain hands\-on experience supporting sustainability initiatives across a global real assets platform, with a primary focus on real estate. You will work closely with the Sustainability team and collaborate with professionals across investment, asset management, and operations functions.


This role offers exposure to how sustainability is integrated throughout the investment lifecycle, from data collection and reporting to asset\-level initiatives and investor communications. You will contribute to meaningful projects while developing a foundational understanding of sustainability practices within the real estate industry.


The ideal candidate is proactive, highly organized, and execution\-focused. They will be comfortable operating with limited direction, synthesizing complex technical information into clear, executive\-level communication, and driving initiatives forward across diverse teams. We are looking for someone who is outcome\-oriented, collaborative, and energized by making things happen across a dynamic global investment platform.


**Strategy \& Integration**

* Support the team in implementing sustainability initiatives across the real estate portfolio
* For new acquisitions, help support sustainability due diligence, including review and quality checks
* Assist with tracking and supporting sustainability initiatives at the asset level

**Data \& Reporting**

* Support sustainability data collection, quality checks, and data ingestion into tool
* Assist with investor reporting and benchmarking, including GRESB and other reporting frameworks (INREV)

**Investor Engagement**

* Complete investor sustainability requests across real estate funds
* Help prepare materials for investor meetings

**Climate Risk \& Regulation**

* Conduct research on sustainability regulations, climate risks, and industry
