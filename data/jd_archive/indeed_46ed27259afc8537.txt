**Who We Are**


Applied Materials is a global leader in materials engineering solutions used to produce virtually every new chip and advanced display in the world. We design, build and service cutting\-edge equipment that helps our customers manufacture display and semiconductor chips – the brains of devices we use every day. As the foundation of the global electronics industry, Applied enables the exciting technologies that literally connect our world – like AI and IoT. If you want to push the boundaries of materials science and engineering to create next generation technology, join us to deliver material innovation that changes the world.

**TEAM OVERVIEW:**

Be part of talented algorithmic team developing vision and ML\-based software and solutions for semiconductor inspection and metrology applications.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Explore traditional image analysis, computer vision and deep learning algorithms for semiconductor wafer defect detection and metrology.
* Work in a team to develop algorithmic software, evaluate and experiment with different solutions.

**TECHNICAL SKILLS:**

* Academic background in computer vision and deep learning solutions for vision applications.
* Proficient in prototyping algorithms in Python and/or C\+\+.
* Strong analytical, problem\-solving skills, work in a team.

**REQUIREMENTS/EDUCATION:**

* Enrolled in a PhD or Master’s degree program in computer science, electrical engineering, or related departments.
* Working experience in OpenCV, PyTorch, able to build and validate ML image models.
* Academic background in signal processing theory is a plus.
