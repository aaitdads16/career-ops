**ABOUT THE POSITION**


VHB’s Watertown, MA office is seeking a Geomatics Intern to join our growing team.

 **Responsibilities**

* Perform various land surveying tasks under the direction of more senior Land Surveyors including: Operation, transport and setup of Total Stations (Manual and Robotic), Data Collectors, GPS, and other Land Surveying equipment.
* Reconnaissance and recovery of Land Surveying markers through use of hand tools, brush cutting, and digging.
* Ability to transport Land Surveying Equipment, navigate and traverse through difficult terrain, environments and wooded areas.
* Performs construction stakeout of proposed improvements, including the installation of stakes through use of sledgehammers and hand tools.
* Maintains proper care of all survey equipment.
* Requires valid Driver's license and ability to operate company vehicles, including large cargo vans.
* Some overnight travel may be required from time to time due to workshare opportunities with other VHB offices.
* Documents all phases of survey work through clear field notes and data collection
* Land surveying including, but not limited to, Boundary, ALTA/NSPS Land Title Surveys, Existing Conditions \& Topographic survey, Construction layout, and utility surveys.

**Skills and Attributes**

* Ability to learn the proper use of GPS equipment and Manual and or Robotic Instruments. Ability to learn mathematical concepts such as the fundamentals of geometry
* Understands the basics of Boundary Survey, Topographic, Construction, and Utility Surveys
* Able to think critically
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with survey staff and clients

**Qualifications**

* Currently enrolled in an undergraduate program (Geomatics, Civil Engineering, Construction Management or related field)
