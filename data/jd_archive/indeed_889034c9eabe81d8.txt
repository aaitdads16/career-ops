**About the Position:**  

This internship offers a hands\-on opportunity to work in Materials R\&D on next\-generation sealing systems for electronic paper displays. The intern will own a focused project to evaluate material interactions between polymer films and fluid formulations that impact coating and device performance.



The role emphasizes experimental design, surface characterization, and data\-driven analysis using lab\-scale methods. The intern will work closely with scientists and engineers while independently driving their project and presenting results at the end of the internship.


**Responsibilities:**


* Prepare and compound polymer formulations using lab\-scale mixing tools (e.g. balances and mixers)
* Support qualification of hand fill\-and\-seal (HFS) equipment, including basic repeatability assessment (e.g., Gauge R\&R)
* Perform surface and interfacial characterization, including surface energy of films, surface tension of fluids, and adhesion testing between material layers
* Conduct microscopy and visual analysis to evaluate film quality, interfacial behavior, and coating defects
* Execute designed experiments (DOE) to screen material combinations
* Collect, organize, and analyze experimental data
* Present findings, correlations, and recommendations to the team
* Maintain safe laboratory practices at all times

  


**Required Qualifications:**

* GPA of 3\.0 or higher

* Strong interest in materials, coatings, or surface science
* Basic data analysis skills (Excel; Python is a plus)
