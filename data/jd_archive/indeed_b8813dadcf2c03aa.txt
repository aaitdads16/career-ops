The role of the ISOL Trainee is distinctive in that it involves rotating through different teams within Magna IT’s (MIT) ISOL sub tower. The incumbent will have the opportunity to be part of various teams, including various SAP teams, Data Interchange \& Development (EDI), and Manufacturing Execution System (MES) teams.

***Major Responsibilities:***

* Administering, support and maintenance of business applications which are operated by teams within ISOL sub towers
* Engage in activities for 1st and 2nd level user support
* Analysis of malfunctions and troubleshooting
* Interface to 3rd level support for issue and problem management
* Manage ticket queue and prioritize work based on impact analysis
* Administration of master data
* Documentation: Prepare technical documentation, keep all internal documentation systems up to date
* Application and user management
* Support for application testing
