About the Job
  
We are looking for an intern to support the local Learning Development team in coordinating and administering training programs. You will work hands\-on with learning management systems, collaborate with both local and global LD specialists and gain real exposure to corporate training operations in an international technology environment.
  
  

What you do
  
  

* Manage the training calendar and set up events in the LMS.
* Publish and promote sessions through internal communication channels.
* Handle on\-site logistics (catering, room setup, materials) and track attendance with signature collection.
* Create learning paths and share resources and links within the company’s LMS tools. Assist employees with platform queries and escalate technical issues to the global support team.
* Verify training eligibility per local regulations, coordinate documentation with organizers and submit records to the compliance team.
* Manage signature collection and archive all documentation per retention policies. Update internal pages and contribute to LD branding content.
* Send satisfaction surveys, analyze feedback and communicate mandatory training deadlines to employees and generate reports on our LMS System.


What you bring
  
  

* Currently studying or recently graduated in Human Resources, Psychology, Business Administration, Education, or a related field.
* Able to sign an internship agreement with a university or training institution.
* Strong Spanish and English (written and spoken).
* Good command of Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint).
* Organized, detail\-oriented, proactive and comfortable managing multiple tasks
* Previous experience in a similar role (LD, HR, or training internships) and familiarity with LMS platforms, virtual meeting tools, or local training compliance frameworks.
* Minimum 6\-month commitment in a part\-time or full\-time environment (hybrid working model).
* Basic knowledge of AI technologies, principles and their practical use.


What we offer
  
  

* A modern and open space office located in Barcelona.
* Flexible working hours and an excellent hybrid working model.
* You will receive a laptop and any material you need for your work.
* Activities nearly every week (afterworks, games night, skating, padel and many more!)
* Job opportunities in a global company.
