About the job

Do you love working with data, systems and digital tools to improve complex global processes?



Are you an analytical thinker with strong Microsoft skills and curiosity about technology, automation and AI in a business context?

  



Then apply now and join us as our Transfer Pricing Transformation Intern in our office in Hoofddorp starting August 2026!

  



In this internship, you will be part of the Global Transfer Pricing \& Tax Transformation team, within our tax / Transfer Pricing function.
