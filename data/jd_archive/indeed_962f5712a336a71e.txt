Internship \- Speak Up and employee behaviour


“Being part of really continuing to keep Heineken a great place to work for everyone by ensuring employees have a safe work environment to thrive, is really inspiring and fun!”



The internship



Come join the Social Sustainability and Human Rights team and contribute to our efforts in creating a positive impact.



Main responsibilities \& projects


* Assist with upskilling the Global People Function on HR related Code of Business Conduct topics like harassment and discrimination.
* Collaborate with the Global People community regarding their role in the follow up of Workplace Grievances.
* Support in the upskilling of our Global Trusted Representatives Community by developing and co\-facilitating online workshops.
* Assist in the upskilling of our People Case Manager Community to improve their Speak Up investigation skills.
* Gather insights to further inform decision making, including benchmarking and trend monitoring.
* Help create communications and messaging on Speak Up HR/People and behavioural topics.


During your internship you will get the opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as a company, also outside your department. We give you the trust and freedom, so you can work like a real entrepreneur. We like to bring the outside world inside and therefore we embrace new, fresh ideas with enthusiasm. Of course, always with the guidance that is right for you.



The department



As an intern you'll be an integral part of the Global Social Sustainability and Human Rights team, which ensures that how HEINEKEN does business in its own operations and across its value chain respects people’s dignity and human rights.



The terms of employment


* Your starting date is 1 September 2026
* The internship is fulltime
* The duration of the internship is 5 or 6 months (in consultation with your internship supervisor)
* It is a practice\-oriented internship
* You will be based in Amsterdam, with a hybrid way of working (3 days office, 2 days at home)
* You will receive an internship fee of €650 gross per month (based on 40 hours per week)
* You will enjoy 2 vacation days per month (based on 40 hours per week)
* If you do not have a student OV product, your commuting expenses will be fully reimbursed


The start of your internship



Your team and the People partner within Global People will support your onboarding. Furthermore, you can connect with other interns doing internship within the Global People function and other department.



The unique you!


* Currently pursuing a degree in social studies, human rights, HR or any related field
* Ability to prioritize and to work in a structured way on projects and individually
* Great team player and eager to learn
* Proficiency in English: written, spoken and interpersonal


We believe that everyone has unique talents and are therefore looking for you!
