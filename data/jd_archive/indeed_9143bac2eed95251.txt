**Position Summary:**  

We have an exciting opportunity to join our team as a Registered Dietitian.
  

  

In this role, the successful candidate Responsible for providing optimum nutritional care to patients admitted to inpatient units or in outpatient clinics. Care provided through nutrition screening, assessment, support, counseling, metabolic monitoring, and medical team communication. Act as an ambassador for nutrition services while delivering world class care.

**Job Responsibilities:**

* Completes initial nutrition assessments and reassessments in accordance with departmental policies and guidelines.
* Develops and implements evidence\-based nutrition care plans tailored to patients medical conditions and needs.
* Evaluates and monitors the effectiveness and outcomes of medical nutrition therapy intervention and follows up as patient needs change.
* Performs nutrition focused physical exams (NFPE) to determine risk and/or degree of malnutrition. Document malnutrition using the PES (problem, etiology, sign/symptoms) format, as applicable.
* Functions as a resource person to interdisciplinary team concerning patients nutritional care.
* Educates patients and families on nutrition recommendations and interventions considering cultural needs and readiness to learn.
* Maintains readiness for regulatory surveys
* Attends interdisciplinary team rounds and/or meetings as assigned.
* Manages parenteral nutrition support as assigned.
* Precepts, counsels and provides training to clinical nutrition staff members, dietetic interns, physician trainees and/or fellows as assigned.
* Participates in continuing education to maintain professional competency and stay current with the latest nutritional research and evidence\-based practices.
* Participates in quality initiatives and data collection for outcome measures as assigned.
* Provides in\-service education within the hospital or community setting as needed or requested.
* Performs other duties assigned.
