**Job Overview**   
We are seeking a motivated and detail\-oriented Project Intern to join our team. This paid internship offers an excellent opportunity for individuals looking to gain practical experience in project management and civil engineering. The successful candidate will support our project teams with various tasks, contributing to the successful delivery of infrastructure projects while developing essential skills for a future career in the industry.   
**Responsibilities**

* Assist in the preparation and updating of project schedules using Primavera P6 software.
* Support the utilisation of Civil 3D for drafting, design, and data organisation tasks.
* Contribute to project documentation, including reports, plans, and technical drawings.
* Coordinate with team members to ensure project deadlines are met through effective time management.
* Attend project meetings and record minutes, action points, and follow\-up activities.
* Conduct research and gather data relevant to ongoing projects.
* Support the team in monitoring project progress and identifying potential issues early.

**Requirements**

* Currently enrolled in a relevant degree programme such as Civil Engineering, Construction Management, or similar.
* Familiarity with Primavera P6 project scheduling software is desirable.
* Experience with Civil 3D or similar civil design software is advantageous.
* Strong organisational skills with excellent time management abilities.
* Good communication skills and ability to work collaboratively within a team environment.
* Demonstrated enthusiasm for civil engineering and project management disciplines. This internship provides valuable hands\-on experience in a professional setting, fostering growth in technical skills and industry knowledge while supporting ongoing projects within our organisation.
