Overview:

A U.S. Department of Energy National Laboratory managed by Princeton University, the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) is tackling the world’s toughest science and technology challenges using plasma, the fourth state of matter. With more than 70 years of history, PPPL is a leader in the science and engineering behind the development of fusion energy, a potentially limitless energy source. PPPL is also using its expertise to advance research in the areas of microelectronics, quantum sensors and devices, and sustainability sciences. Whether it be through science, engineering, technology or professional services, every team member has an opportunity to contribute to our mission and vision. Come join us!
Responsibilities:
**Core Duties:*** This assignment will focus on advancing research in artificial intelligence and machine learning applied to edge\-to\-exascale computing environments. Specifically, the work will involve investigating the incorporation of real\-time dynamic data into the ROSE surrogate building framework for the selection, optimization, and concurrent refresh of an ensemble of models, and designing adaptive system architectures that dynamically select appropriate models based on real\-time conditions and resource availability.
