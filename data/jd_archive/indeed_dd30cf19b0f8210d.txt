**Title and Summary**


Climate Resilience Manager
Overview of Role  

As the Climate Resilience Manager at Mastercard, you will be a key contributor within our Real Estate Services division, reporting directly to the Physical Climate Risk and Resiliency Director. This role is primarily focused on identifying, implementing, and tracking the effectiveness of climate resilience projects across our real estate portfolio. You will lead efforts to enhance our portfolio's resilience to climate\-related risks, both current and future, by leveraging innovative approaches, including nature\-based solutions.  

In this role, you will play a crucial part in shaping Mastercard’s overall climate resilience strategy. This includes ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and policies and driving the adoption of best practices across the organization. You will also collaborate extensively with interdisciplinary teams—such as Workplace Safety, Enterprise Resilience, and Enterprise Risk Management—and forge strategic external partnerships aimed at bolstering the resilience and safety of our employees and the communities we serve.  

Your success in this role will require excellent presentation and communication skills to effectively convey complex climate risk and adaptation information to both internal and external stakeholders. You will also need to think creatively about climate resilience strategies, integrating nature\-based solutions where applicable.  

  

Role:  

* Identify, implement, and track climate resilience projects across the real estate portfolio, focusing on long\-term risks.
* Collaborate with cross\-departmental teams to develop and execute strategies that address climate\-related challenges, emphasizing nature\-based solutions to enhance community resilience.
* Forge and maintain external partnerships with community organizations, government agencies, and other stakeholders to strengthen the security and safety of our employees and the communities we serve.
* Stay informed about emerging trends and best practices in climate resilience, sustainability, and nature\-based solutions.
* Support the Physical Climate Risk and Resiliency Director in the development and implementation of policies and procedures related to climate resilience.
