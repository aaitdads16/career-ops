Internship – Gen AI Change \& Communications



“Being part of a GenAI product that people actually use across the company has been an amazing learning experience. You really see how your work makes an impact.”



The internship



Hoppy is HEINEKEN’s GenAI chatbot for reporting and knowledge management, used across global teams, regions Operation Companies (OpCos) and HEINEKEN Business Services (HBS) hubs. As a Change \& Communication Intern, you will support the adoption of GenAI by translating technology into clear messages, engaging users, and helping teams embrace new ways of working.



During this internship, you will:


* Support change management and adoption of Hoppy across the organisation
* Create and coordinate internal communications such as updates, announcements, and onboarding materials
* Translate GenAI developments and industry trends into clear value for HEINEKEN users
* Help define value propositions for different user groups (BI, business, OpCos)
* Support community building through key users, ambassadors, and feedback loops
* Collaborate closely with the Product Owner, BI teams, and Business Insights stakeholders


This internship offers hands\-on experience at the intersection of technology, communication, and organisational change.



Your new team and workplace



You will be part of the Business Insights \& Process Orchestraion department, which owns centralised BI and reporting capabilities within HEINEKEN. As part of our acceleration, Hoppy is being developed as a GenAI\-powered solution to support insights and knowledge sharing at scale.



The team consists of 40\+ skilled professionals working on BI, data, and GenAI solutions in a highly collaborative and international environment. You’ll have the freedom to work entrepreneurially, bring in fresh ideas, and make a visible impact, while always receiving the right level of guidance.



During your internship, you’ll have every opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as an organisation, not just within your own team, but across departments. We trust and empower you to work with an entrepreneurial mindset. At HEINEKEN, we love bringing the outside world in and welcome fresh, new ideas with open arms. Of course, we’ll make sure you get the guidance that suits you.



The unique you!



We believe everyone has unique talents, which is why we’re looking for you! Energy, drive and the ambition to grow are what make the difference at HEINEKEN. In addition, you bring:


* Are currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program
* Have an interest in GenAI, change management, communication, or digital transformation
* Enjoy explaining complex topics in a clear and engaging way
* Are comfortable with basic tools like PowerPoint and Excel
* Like working with different stakeholders and collaborating in teams


Even if you feel you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply. An internship is the perfect opportunity to learn and grow!
