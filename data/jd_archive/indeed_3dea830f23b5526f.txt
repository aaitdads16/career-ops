**About the Role**

We are looking for motivated and curious Polytechnic students to join PwC’s Cyber Security Audit team as Assistant Associate. This role offers an opportunity to gain hands\-on experience in cyber security, audit processes, risk assessment, and technology\-driven improvement initiatives within a professional services environment.


In addition to supporting the team’s day\-to\-day audit and cyber\-related activities, interns will also have the opportunity to explore how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automation tools can be used to improve productivity, streamline workflows, and enhance the quality of our work.


**Key Responsibilities**

As a Cyber Security Audit Assistant Associate , you may be involved in the following:


* Support the team in performing cyber security audit and risk assessment activities
* Assist in documenting audit procedures, findings, and recommendations
* Help review IT systems, processes, and controls to identify gaps or areas for improvement
* Conduct research on cyber security trends, risks, frameworks, and best practices
* Assist with data gathering, analysis, and preparation of audit working papers and reports
* Explore opportunities to use AI tools and automation to improve internal work processes
* Help develop simple AI\-enabled solutions, scripts, dashboards, or workflow improvements to reduce manual effort
* Contribute ideas to enhance team efficiency, reporting quality, and knowledge sharing
* Work closely with team members on special projects related to cyber security, audit innovation, and process transformation
