**Job Description:**  



**About the role**
------------------

Uniphore's Agentic Process Discovery platform enables enterprises to automatically understand how their teams work — ingesting screen recordings, videos, and transcripts to map and codify business processes at scale. As an intern on this team, you'll contribute directly to next\-generation enhancements of this capability, working alongside AI researchers and product engineers on real, production\-grade challenges.


**What you'll work on**
-----------------------

* Improve the platform's ability to ingest and interpret multi\-modal data sources — video, screen recordings, and audio transcripts — to produce richer process models.
* Explore and prototype techniques for deeper process understanding, such as intent recognition, workflow segmentation, and procedural summarization.
* Contribute to pipelines that translate discovered process knowledge into automation workflows and training data for small language models (SLMs).
* Collaborate with product, data science, and automation engineering teams to ensure process artifacts are structured for maximum downstream utility.
* Help define evaluation frameworks and quality benchmarks for process discovery outputs.
What we're looking for


* Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or a related field (BS, MS, or PhD).
* Coursework or project experience with machine learning, NLP, computer vision, or multi\-modal systems.
* Familiarity with Python and common ML frameworks (PyTorch, HuggingFace, etc.).
* Exposure to LLMs, SLMs, or fine\-tuning workflows is a strong plus. **\+**
* Interest in process mining, workflow analysis, or RPA concepts is a bonus. **\+**
* Strong curiosity, collaborative mindset, and comfort working in a fast\-moving applied research environment.
