This is a hands\-on, high\-impact role for someone who thrives on variety, takes pride in flawless execution, and loves working in a fast\-moving, people\-first environment.

You'll support the planning and delivery of a calendar of recruiting events and campus activations across EMEA, including flagship conferences and office visits. You'll also coordinate ad\-hoc office tours throughout the year.

You'll keep things running smoothly behind the scenes, managing on\-going administrative processes, PO and contract management, coordinating swag orders, and providing general programme support.
