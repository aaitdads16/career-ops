**A Snapshot of Your Day**


Join the Gas Services Solutions team in Singapore for a 6\-month internship supporting digital transformation initiatives, including SharePoint data migration, application and user support, and Power BI dashboard development. You'll gain hands\-on experience in data preparation, visualization, and digital tool adoption while collaborating with cross\-functional teams in a professional engineering environment.  

  

**How You’ll Make an Impact**

* Support the planning, organization, and execution of data migration from a legacy shared drive to a new platform (60%)
* Conduct post\-migration verification to ensure data integrity, folder structures, and user permissions are correctly implemented
* Coordinate with team members to schedule and manage the migration of their respective datasets
* Provide first\-level technical support to team members on common software applications and IT systems (40%)
* Develop and maintain support documentation, including user guides, knowledge base articles, and FAQs
* Contribute to Power BI reporting by assisting with data preparation, validation, and dashboard development

**What You Bring**

* Currently pursuing a Diploma or Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or equivalent
* No prior professional experience required; academic projects or demonstrated interest in IT support and data management are advantageous
* Familiarity with data migration, file management, and basic troubleshooting
* Exposure to Power BI or other data visualization tools, and ability to create clear technical documentation
* Strong attention to detail, problem\-solving mindset, and effective communication skills
* Good time management and adaptability in a dynamic, collaborative environment
