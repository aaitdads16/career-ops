**Site Overview**
-----------------

  

Established in 2000, the John F. Welch Technology Center (JFWTC) in Bengaluru is our multidisciplinary research and engineering center. Engineers and scientists at JFWTC have contributed to hundreds of aviation patents, pioneering breakthroughs in engine technologies, advanced materials, and additive manufacturing.

**Job Description:**


Seeking an intern with computer science background with knowledge of Java, JavaScript, and web development to support software design and development activities.


The intern will assist in building, testing, and maintaining web applications, while collaborating with cross\-functional teams on feature implementation and debugging.  

Candidates should have a basic understanding of front\-end and back\-end development concepts, problem\-solving skills, and a willingness to learn in a fast\-paced environment.  

This internship offers an opportunity to gain hands\-on experience in real\-world software projects and enhance technical skills in modern development practices.  

Basic familiarity with AI/ML concepts or hands\-on exposure to Generative AI tools, prompt engineering, or integrating AI\-powered features/APIs into web applications is preferred.

Candidates with pursuing B.Tech should apply.

* Currently enrolled in a B.Tech program
