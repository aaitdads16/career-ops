At Keros, our mission is to deliver significant clinical benefit to a wide range of patients with disorders that are linked to dysfunctional signaling of the transforming growth factor\-beta (TGF\-ß) family of proteins. With a focus on developing differentiated product candidates that are designed to alter TGF\-β signaling, we aim to target the pathways critical for the growth, repair and maintenance of blood cells and a number of tissues, including bone, skeletal muscle, adipose and heart tissue. We believe our product candidates have the potential to unlock the full therapeutic benefits of modulating the TGF\-β superfamily and provide disease\-modifying benefit to patients.

**Job Summary:** The Corporate Strategy Intern will assist the Corporate Strategy team in evaluating high\-impact growth opportunities within the biopharma sector. You will contribute to projects that shape the company's long\-term portfolio, from early\-stage drug discovery through commercialization.


**Responsibilities:**


* Support the evaluation of internal R\&D assets and potential M\&A targets by analyzing clinical trial data and investment economics.
* Monitor competitor drug portfolios, patent expirations, and regulatory milestones (e.g., FDA/EMA approvals) to provide weekly insights to leadership.
* Conduct deep dives into industry trends such as personalized medicine, AI\-driven drug discovery, and digital therapeutics.
* Work with members of the Corporate Strategy team to build a roadmap that summarizes the areas where we can use AI to be more effective in our day\-to\-day work.
* Partner with R\&D, Regulatory, Clinical Development, Manufacturing, and Finance teams to align on corporate growth objectives.


**Required Qualifications:**


* Currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in Business, Economics, Finance, or a Life Science field (Biology, Chemistry, Pharmacy).
* Proficiency in Excel for data analysis and PowerPoint for creating executive\-ready presentations.
* A strong desire to understand the life sciences industry and the global regulatory framework (FDA, EMA).
* Ability to synthesize complex scientific and clinical data into clear business recommendations.
* Candidate must have prior experience working in a consulting firm or a healthcare\-related business environment.
