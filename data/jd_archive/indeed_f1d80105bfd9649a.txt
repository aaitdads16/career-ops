“During my internship, I felt genuinely appreciated as an integral team member, within an environment that actively encouraged learning and growth!”



The internship



Come join the Social Sustainability team and contribute to our efforts in creating a positive impact.



Main responsibilities \& projects


* Assist in the development and implementation of the social sustainability value chain due diligence strategy, ensuring alignment with company objectives and values.
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams to support due diligence programs and initiatives.
* Gather insights to further inform decision making, including benchmarking and legal trend monitoring.
* Help create communications and messaging on social sustainability topics.


During your internship you will get the opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as a company, also outside your department. We give you the trust and freedom, so you can work like a real entrepreneur. We like to bring the outside world inside and therefore we embrace new, fresh ideas with enthusiasm. Of course always with the guidance that is right for you.



The department



As an intern you'll be an integral part of the Global Social Sustainability team, which ensures that how HEINEKEN does business in its own operations and across its value chain respects people’s dignity and human rights.



The terms of employment


* Your starting date is 1 September 2026
* The internship is fulltime (in consultation also 4 days a week possible)
* The duration of the internship is 6 months
* It is a practice\-oriented internship
* You will be based in Amsterdam
* During your internship, you have the flexibility to work in a hybrid way of working
* You will receive an internship fee of €650 gross per month based on 40 hours per week
* You will enjoy 2 vacation days per month (based on fulltime)
* If you do not have a student OV product, your commuting expenses will be fully reimbursed


The unique you!



We believe that everyone has unique talents and are therefore looking for you!


* Currently pursuing a degree in sustainability, social studies, human rights or any related field
* Ability to prioritize and to work in a structured way on projects and individually
* Exceptional team player and eager to learn
* Proficiency in English: written, spoken and interpersonal
