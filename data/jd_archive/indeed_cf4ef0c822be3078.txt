Internship – Internal Communications \& Engagement (Supply Chain Sustainability)



“Creating engaging stories that connect people and bring sustainability to life across HEINEKEN’s operations.”



The internship



HEINEKEN is committed to driving sustainability and operational excellence across its global breweries. To support this ambition, we are looking for a creative and proactiveintern to strengthen internal communications and employee engagement within our Global Sustainable Production team



In this internship, you will help build and maintain clear, engaging communication channels, by creating high\-quality digital content, managing key internal platforms such as SharePoint and Workvivo, and supporting the communication agenda of strategic sustainability programs like Race to Reduce. You will work closely with the team to simplify complex topics, improve how we share information, and bring our sustainability impact to life through strong storytelling.



In this internship you will be responsible for:


* Supporting internal communications, employee engagement, and change management initiatives.
* Helping manage the communications calendar and maintaining key digital channels (Workvivo, SharePoint), ensuring content is timely, structured, and easy to navigate.
* Developing people\-focused digital content, including visuals, short videos, and storytelling assets to showcase how Sustainable Production supports breweries in achieving sustainability goals
* Assisting in the creation of communication materials for presentations, reports, and community webinars, ensuring alignment with HEINEKEN’s visual identity and tone of voice.
* Collaborating with Global Sustainable Production colleagues to ensure consistency, quality, and impact of communication outputs


Focus areas (where you will lead and propose improvements)



SharePoint – structure, usability, and content experience


* Support the project to update and improve our team SharePoint: propose a clearer structure, better navigation, and a more useful content experience for the audience.
* Help create a well\-structured SharePoint page that enables easy access to key content, organized by topic/area and aligned with how users search for information.
* Bring ideas to make content more intuitive and easier to use.


Workvivo – engagement and communication rhythm


* Support our Workvivo channel by planning and publishing content, keeping a consistent rhythm, and improving engagement through strong storytelling and visuals.
* Create content formats that employees actually want to read and share.


Race to Reduce – storytelling and program communications


* Support the Race to Reduce communications agenda across channels (Workvivo, SharePoint, dashboards update)
* Strengthen internal storytelling around Race to Reduce to inform, inspire, and encourage participation
* Help design a refreshed approach to sharing winner stories that drives motivation and participation.
