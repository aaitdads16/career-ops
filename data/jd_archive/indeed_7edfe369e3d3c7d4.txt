**Overview**  

  

Alpha and Omega Semiconductor (AOS) is exploring how artificial intelligence can enhance productivity and operational efficiency across a global semiconductor organization. We are early in this journey and intentionally taking a measured approach given the risks associated with data security, confidentiality, and AI model training.


This internship is designed for someone who can help us answer a fundamental question: *Where should we start with AI\-and how do we do it responsibly?*

**Duration:** Summer 2026, 10–12 weeks

**Key Responsibilities**

* Research practical AI use cases relevant to a semiconductor environment (e.g., Sales, HR, engineering workflows, manufacturing support, and corporate functions)
* Analyze how peer companies and leading organizations are deploying AI tools while protecting confidential and proprietary information
* Evaluate AI platforms (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, enterprise copilots) with a focus on data privacy, model training risk, and enterprise controls
* Identify "low\-risk, high\-impact" opportunities for AI adoption within AOS
* Develop guidelines and recommendations for safe and compliant use of AI, including guardrails around sensitive data
* Liaise with external AI vendors/partners to understand capabilities, limitations, and security frameworks
* Synthesize findings into clear, executive\-ready recommendations, including a proposed starting roadmap

**Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing a degree in Business, HR, Engineering, Data Science, or related field
* Strong research and critical thinking skills; able to separate hype from practical application
* Interest in AI with an appreciation for data privacy, security, and compliance considerations
* Ability to communicate complex topics in a clear, business\-oriented way
* Comfortable navigating ambiguity\-we are building the playbook as we go
