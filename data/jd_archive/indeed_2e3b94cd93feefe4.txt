**Job Responsibilities**

* Assists in project scoping and strategic planning for deal analytics engagements.
* Researches and analyzes business and financial data to develop insights aligned to transaction objectives.
* Oversees execution of analytical workflows and supports development of client deliverables.
* Applies critical thinking and problem\-solving skills to address complex analytical questions.
* Collaborates with cross\-functional teams to integrate analytical findings into deal insights.
* Supports coaching and review of junior team members’ work.

* Bachelor’s degree in accounting, business, economics, mathematics, computer science, or a related field.
* 4–7 years of experience.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience supporting M\&A or transaction\-related analytics engagements.
* Strong communication and stakeholder\-management skills.
