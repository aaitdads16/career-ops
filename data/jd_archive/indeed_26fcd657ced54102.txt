**The mission of this role**



Support Industry 4\.0 and factory automation projects, gaining hands\-on experience in connecting industrial systems with digital platforms. This role offers exposure to automation, industrial communications, IoT, data analytics and dashboards, while contributing to real operational use cases in manufacturing environments.

 **Key Responsibilities**

  

* Contribute to Industry 4\.0 and industrial automation projects, connecting shopfloor systems (PLCs, SCADA) with digital platforms.
* Support the implementation of industrial communications and IoT solutions (OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, edge devices and gateways).
* Develop and maintain data integration and automation scripts using Python, SQL and Linux environments.
* Assist in the creation of industrial KPIs, operational dashboards and monitoring systems.
* Collaborate on digital transformation projects, documenting solutions and working with OT, IT and business teams.
