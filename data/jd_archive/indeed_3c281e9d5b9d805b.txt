The **Process Engineer Summer Intern** will support the Process Engineering team in the development, qualification, and optimization of semiconductor manufacturing processes and capital equipment. This role provides hands\-on exposure to semiconductor process technologies, cleanroom operations, and equipment qualification activities.
The intern will work closely with experienced engineers to analyze process performance, support tool setup and validation, and assist in continuous improvement initiatives. This internship offers a valuable opportunity to gain practical experience in semiconductor manufacturing environments and capital equipment development.


**Essential Duties and Responsibilities*** Assist Process Engineers with **setup, qualification, and optimization of semiconductor manufacturing tools and processes**
* Support **process characterization and validation activities**, including Design of Experiments (DOE) and data analysis
* Participate in **tool installation, startup, and process qualification activities**
* Collect and analyze process data related to yield, throughput, uniformity, and defect performance
* Assist in performing **process troubleshooting and root cause analysis** related to equipment or process issues
* Support development and documentation of **process recipes, work instructions, and qualification reports**
* Assist with **tool and process acceptance testing** (FAT/SAT where applicable)
* Participate in **continuous improvement initiatives** to enhance process capability and equipment performance
* Work with cross\-functional teams including Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Manufacturing, and Quality
* Maintain accurate engineering records and technical documentation
* Follow all **cleanroom protocols, ESD controls, and safety procedures**

**Learning Objectives**During the internship, the Process Engineer Intern will gain experience in:* Semiconductor manufacturing processes
* Cleanroom operations and protocols
* Capital equipment setup and qualification
* Process troubleshooting and yield improvement
* Statistical process control (SPC) and data analysis
* Engineering documentation and reporting
* Cross\-functional collaboration in semiconductor equipment environments
