**Internship Overview**
-----------------------


**Duration:** 3 months
  

**Starting date:** 2026/2027
  

**Location:** Barcelona, Spain (hybrid work model with required on\-site presence)
  

  

Dolby's Barcelona office hosts world\-class audio Research and Development teams specializing in spatial audio, AI, and signal processing for both professional and consumer applications. As a Sound Experiences Lab Intern, you'll join our research team to develop cutting\-edge AI solutions for spatial audio processing.  

  

  



**Responsibilities**
--------------------


* Design, implement and train advanced deep learning models for spatial audio processing tasks, leveraging self\-supervised learning, multi\-modal learning, etc.
* Prepare and curate high\-quality datasets through data augmentation and synthetic data generation.
* Evaluate proposed models against state\-of\-the\-art research benchmarks.
* Collaborate closely with Barcelona's research team on related projects.
* Present research findings and contribute to patent applications and scientific publications.
  

  

  

**Minimum Qualifications**
--------------------------


* **Education:** Master degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics or similar.
* **Current Status:** Enrolled in a PhD program on subjects related to audio processing and AI for the entire duration of the internship (February 2026 to June 2026\). Master students with a research publication track may also be considered for the position.
* Proven experience in audio signal processing.
* Hands\-on experience applying AI to audio applications.
* Proficiency in at least one deep learning framework using Python.
* Strong mathematical foundation.
* Experience in research, reflected in research publications.
* Good communication skills.
  

  

  

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------


* Master degree in a subject related to audio processing.
* Knowledge of spatial audio.
* Knowledge of audio\-related AI models.
* Critical listening skills.
* Proven track of publication records in the field of audio, acoustics, and machine learning.
