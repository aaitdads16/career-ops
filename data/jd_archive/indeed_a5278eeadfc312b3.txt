Our teams combine Nordic values with Indian expertise to deliver innovative, scalable, and high\-impact solutions.  

We're proud to be recognized as a **Great Place to Work**, a testament to our inclusive culture, strong leadership, and commitment to employee well\-being and growth. At Tarento, you’ll be part of a collaborative environment where ideas are valued, learning is continuous, and careers are built on passion and purpose. **About the Role**
We welcome students from design and visual communication backgrounds to intern with our design team as part of their academic internship requirement. Whether you are here for a summer internship, an industry internship, a graduation project, or simply exploring opportunities in the design field, you will work alongside experienced designers on real projects — getting hands\-on exposure to UX, UI, and visual design in a professional product environment. Regardless of the internship type, you will work on real design problems, receive structured mentorship, and leave with a stronger portfolio and a clearer sense of what product design looks like in practice.
**What You Will Do**
* Contribute to end\-to\-end product design work — from understanding user problems and mapping flows to designing interfaces that are both functional and visually considered
* Think through design decisions in context — who the user is, what they are trying to do, and how the interface supports or hinders that
* Create visual assets and graphic design elements aligned with brand and design system guidelines
* Participate in design reviews and critique sessions, contributing your perspective and receiving structured feedback
* Support documentation and handoff preparation
* Explore AI\-assisted design tools as part of day\-to\-day ideation and iteration

**Who Should Apply**
* Students currently enrolled in B.Des, B.FTech, BFA, or equivalent design programs
* Open to students from NIFT, NID, design schools, and visual communication programs
* Completion certificate and internship letter provided upon successful completion

**What We Are Looking For**
* A design portfolio or academic project work that shows you can think through a problem, not just style a screen
* Ability to connect visual decisions to user needs — why something is designed a certain way, not just how it looks
* Some awareness of product context — understanding that design exists within a larger product, business, and user ecosystem
* Basic understanding of UX principles — layout, hierarchy, user flows, and usability
* Graphic design sensibility — typography, colour, and visual composition
* Working knowledge of Figma or Adobe
