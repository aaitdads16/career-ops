**Job Responsibilities :**
--------------------------


We are looking for a Data Scientist Intern to support the building of AI applications through code


optimization, algorithm research, and algorithm development. This role is ideal for candidates who


are passionate about translating cutting\-edge AI research into scalable, real\-world products—with


a particular interest in enhancing experiences for gamers.


You will collaborate closely with cross\-functional teams across AI, data engineering, product


development, and marketing to design, prototype, and deploy intelligent systems that drive user


engagement and business impact. This is a unique opportunity to contribute meaningfully to AI


product innovation in a dynamic, fast\-paced environment.

**Key responsibilities:**

* Perform discovery and research to enhance application performance
* Quantify implementation choices with product or business\-related considerations
* Implement unit tests for independent modules and services
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams to prototype, iterate, and deploy AI\-driven solutions
* Contribute to codebases using version control best practices via GitHub
* Documentation of methodologies and codebase for knowledge sharing, general understanding,


and collaboration

**What we are looking for:**

* Proficiency in Python and familiarity with Object Oriented Programming
* Familiarity with Large Language Models (LLMs) — including their utilization, common limitations,


and evaluation techniques

* Understanding of context and memory management in LLMs and/or agentic workflows
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills to collaborate eﬀectively with both technical


and non\-technical stakeholders

* Experience using GitHub for version control, issue tracking, pull requests, code reviews, and


collaborative development workflows

* Passion for gaming
* Comfortable working in fast\-paced, high\-pressure, agile environment

**What will set you apart from others:**

* Hands\-on experience with databases such as PostgreSQL, Redis, and Neo4j
* Familiarity with messaging services/protocols like HTTP, MQTT, and AMQP
* Understanding of or experience developing stateless systems

**Qualifications:**

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or


a related technical discipline
