**OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENT:**  

The Fleet Technologies Group leads a portfolio of innovative projects focused on enhancing operations, customer communications, and depot operations.  

  

The Fleet Technologies Group has deployed Bus Hardware Subsystems (BHS) across all MTA buses to support the Automatic Vehicle Location (AVL) system. These onboard devices not only transmit periodic location and log\-on data but also act as wireless gateways for other onboard systems. The intern will play a critical part in daily operations by reviewing system reports, bus block logs, and other diagnostic data to identify BHS units require maintenance visits. They'll track Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) card installations across the fleet and manage updates to Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, and Safety (RAMS) data for the BHS, which feed quarterly compliance and audit reports. The intern will also coordinate with vendors and internal MTA teams to resolve edge case repairs.  

  

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**  

* BHS Repair Review – Assess daily automated reports covering roughly 300 flagged units to identify buses needing maintenance.
* Non\-Automated Data Aggregation – Gather supplemental data from Road Operations, SPEAR database, depot communications, and other channels to identify other failed units.
* Daily Priority List Preparation – Consolidate data to compile a prioritized and well\-organized list for vendor maintenance visits.
* Repair Reconciliation – Compare and track vendor work reports against the prior day’s list.
* Edge Case Coordination – Identify, schedule and verify special repair cases (e.g., antenna replacements) with vendors and depots.
* BHS Unit/SIM Tracking – Maintain an up\-to\-date database mapping BHS units and SIM cards to specific buses.
* SIM Card Operations – Assist with replacement, activation, and deactivation of SIM cards, typically for New Buses.
* RAMS Reporting – Prepare monthly and quarterly Reliability / Availability / Maintainability (RAMS) reports to evaluate system performance and vendor compliance with contracted Service level Agreements (SLA’s).
