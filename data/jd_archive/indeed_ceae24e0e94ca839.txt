Draper is an independent, nonprofit research and development company headquartered in Cambridge, MA. **Job Description Summary:**


Students will have the opportunity to collaborate with engineers to support project development lifecycle from Planning, Requirements Design, Built, Test and Delivery phases of product that meet Draper Quality Management System (QMS), ISO 9001:2015 and Customer specific requirements.  

The individual will work with task lead and team of engineers, developers on test requirements, manufacturing process and product, and test equipment.**Job Description:**

**Duties/Responsibilities**
---------------------------

* Design, develop, and enhancement engineering solutions for challenging problems.
