**THE ROLE**

The Hospitality and Merchandise Strategy and Inventory Analytics Intern will support a focused assortment optimization and inventory productivity project for a key Legends Global account. The work will help identify opportunities to improve SKU productivity, inventory turns, cash flow, storage utilization and assortment decision making.


The Intern will aggregate and validate sales, inventory, margin, and SKU\-level data; help develop an A/B/C stratification model; identify underperforming inventory and high value assortment opportunities; and convert the analysis into actionable insights for operators and merchandise leaders.


The goal is to build a repeatable and reproduceable process that can be standardized across the broader Legends Global Merchandise portfolio.

**ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS**

* Aggregate and organize SKU\-level sales, inventory, margin and sell\-through data
* Support creation of an A/B/C SKU stratification framework
* Identify slow\-moving inventory, over assorted categories, high\-performing products, and cash\-flow improvement opportunities
* Assist in building dashboards, scorecards and reporting tools
* Partner with merchandising, operations, finance, and account teams to validate findings
* Document the process so it can be standardized and scaled across the portfolio
* Prepare summaries and insights for leadership review
