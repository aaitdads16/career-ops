**Intern, Process Sciences in Hopkinton, MA**

Build your future at Curia, where our work has the power to save lives.


The Intern in Downstream Process Development Science will support laboratory activities related to protein purification and bioprocess development. This role is designed for undergraduate students seeking hands‑on experience in an industrial biopharmaceutical laboratory. The intern will work closely with experienced scientists to learn fundamental downstream processing techniques while contributing to ongoing development projects.


Curia is a global contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) with over 30 years of experience partnering with pharmaceutical and biotech companies to provide life\-saving treatments to patients. At Curia, we are on a mission to advance our customers’ therapies from curiosity to cure and ultimately to improve patients’ lives.

**Responsibilities**

* Assist with routine protein purification experiments under supervision
* Learn and support chromatography techniques used in biologics manufacturing (e.g., affinity and ion exchange chromatography)
* Help perform filtration‑related experiments (e.g., depth filtration or membrane filtration)
* Conduct basic laboratory analyses such as SDS‑PAGE and assist with sample preparation
* Prepare buffers, solutions, and reagents following written protocols
* Accurately record experimental observations and data in lab notebooks
* Support general laboratory organization, equipment cleaning, and inventory management
* Follow all safety, quality, and documentation procedures
* Communicate progress and questions to supervising scientists

	+ Biotechnology
	+ Or a related STEM field

**Preferred**
