 **The role**


Your role as CCA is to ensure that our senior leaders effectively communicate with their teams and internal stakeholders. As CCA you will showcase our creative skills and technical know\-how to elevate our internal communications to be truly best in class

**Day to day you will do this by:**

* **Leadership Support**. You will work alongside our leaders and enable them to engage employees and communicate effectively with internal stakeholders. This involves crafting high\-quality communication materials like presentations and videos
* **Create** **Compelling Content**. Using your storytelling skills, you will create engaging, visually appealing, and high\-quality assets for presentations, videos, newsletters, business summaries and all\-hands meetings etc
* **Analyse Impact.** You will be monitor and evaluate your communication’s effectiveness and provide insights for improvements.
* **Collaborate and innovate.** You will work with peers to innovate and enhance best in class communication practices within the entire Digital Hub
