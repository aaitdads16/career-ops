**Job Description**


We are seeking a researcher with strong data science and coding skills to support a cybersecurity training and workforce development project focused on critical infrastructure in the NJ/NY region. The researcher will help organize, analyze, and visualize trainee application data, survey responses, training participation records, and assessment results. The researcher will also support the development of simple data workflows, dashboards, and documentation to help the project team track recruitment, training progress, participant outcomes, and program impact.


Requirements:  

Currently enrolled in a PhD program in Mathematics or a closely related field  

Demonstrated experience in data science and cybersecurity  

Proficiency in at least one programming/analysis tool (e.g., Python, R, Excel, Google Sheets/Forms, or similar platforms), preferably all
