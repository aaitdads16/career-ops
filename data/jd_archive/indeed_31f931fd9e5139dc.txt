Your Opportunity Starts Here.


**Why Join**

Be part of the team that powers sales performance and business growth through data and analytics. As a Sales Performance \& Sales Incentives Intern within CFS Finance, you’ll gain hands‑on experience working with large, complex datasets and applying analytics and AI to solve real business problems. This internship offers the opportunity to contribute to data‑driven decision‑making while gaining exposure to how finance partners the business to drive productivity and outcomes.


**How you succeed**

To succeed in this role, you’ll be analytical, curious, and comfortable working with data. You’ll enjoy digging into numbers, identifying patterns, and translating insights into practical recommendations. Your willingness to learn new tools and collaborate with stakeholders across teams will help you deliver meaningful contributions to projects and initiatives.


**What you do**

Your responsibilities will include:


* **Data Migration Support:** assist with the migration of existing processes and datasets to a new database environment
* **Sales Productivity Analysis:** analyse the productivity of sales staff to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities to improve performance
* **Strategy Support:** support the formulation of strategies and initiatives to drive sales productivity and effectiveness
* **AI Use Cases:** develop and implement AI‑enabled use cases to address business challenges and enhance analytics capabilities
* **Insights \& Reporting:** support analysis and preparation of insights to inform stakeholders and decision‑making

**Who you work with**

You’ll be part of the CFS Finance team, working closely with sales, performance management, analytics, and technology stakeholders across Global Consumer Financial Services. The team plays a critical role in enabling business performance through financial insights, incentive frameworks, and data‑driven analysis.
