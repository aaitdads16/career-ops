### **About the Team**



The Platform Team is responsible for smoothly orchestrating the software at Gravis. We build the observability, profiling, and infrastructure that let engineers understand and optimize software performance across our entire development fleet, from onboard machines to the cloud services coordinating them.



We partner with teams across the company to investigate issues, eliminate bottlenecks, and establish performance best practices. By strengthening core onboard components and providing tools to quickly detect, diagnose, and resolve regressions, we give the rest of Gravis a solid foundation for running our products at scale and the confidence to ship faster.



### **What You'll Work On**


As a Software Engineer intern, you'll focus on Observability \& Monitoring, taking real ownership of tooling that the whole company depends on. Your work includes:
* Owning our Prometheus / Grafana / OpenTelemetry stack
* Setting clear, measurable targets across critical paths (e.g. terrain streaming, latency measurements, gRPC reliability, etc.)
* Building dashboards for machine health, fleet overview, and worksite productivity metrics (e.g. cut/fill volume over time)
* Designing the on\-call alerting pipeline that connects signals from the Rack all the way to the cloud
* Proactively explore and analyze system data to identify trends and anomalies
