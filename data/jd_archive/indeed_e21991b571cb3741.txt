**OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENT:**  

The Long Island Rail Road (LIRR) Office of The Fire Marshal (OFM) is responsible for ensuring the safety of LIRR customers, employees, and vendors and to reduce or eliminate operational impact, property damage and economic loss due to fire or related hazards. These goals are achieved through enforcement of the NYS Fire Code on all LIRR properties and projects in compliance with 19 NYCRR Part 1204; Delivering education and hands on training related to fire safety and rail road safety for LIRR employees and local police / fire / EMS first responders; responding on a 24/7 basis to all LIRR related emergencies acting as a liaison between LIRR and external emergency response agencies; and conducting investigations to the origin and cause of all fires occurring on LIRR property. Additionally, OFM plans and facilitates an annual full scale multi\-agency exercise with the fire service simulating a rail incident and practicing interagency communication, coordination, and safe evacuation of passengers from rail equipment.  

  

The Fire Marshal intern supports implementation of fire safety and prevention programs by assisting with fire safety inspections, updating of building pre\-plan data, assisting with hot work permit inspections, assisting with fire and evacuation drills. Performs other office tasks as assigned.
