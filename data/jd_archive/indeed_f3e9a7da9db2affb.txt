The internship



We are a trailblazing beer brand, renowned for our coolness and popularity among young adult consumers. Our unique fusion of beer with Latin spirit has revolutionized the category making us pioneers. The essence of Latin spirit \- daring, vibrant, authentic, spontaneous and fun is at our core. This grants us the credentials to create the best letting loose moments.



How will you make an impact?


* Digital \& Social Media
* Improve the way we organize the day\-to\-day activities of Desperados social media platforms.
* Develop the monthly calendar for our social platforms and cascade it to our markets.
* Work with our social media agency to find best practices in our operating countries. Leverage the community learnings and reapply in other markets.
* Pilot social media innovations and improve how we communicate with our consumers.
* Event Sponsorship Management
* Manage our worldwide calendar of sponsorships.
* Work with markets to amplify our global partnerships and global activations.
* Build communication plans and assets for our global party sponsorships. Leverage it in different countries
* Event production: We are pushing the frontiers of experimentation in party and we follow closely how the event space is developing. You will be supporting in bringing events to life, in collaboration with our partners globally or in markets.
* Internal conference: Support the brand team for the company’s annual conference in developing \& coordinating the event
* Desperados Team Support and \& Internal Communications
* Become the go\-to individual for accessing our global tools (our internal website, our assets platform)
