**Role and Responsibilities:** *Under the direction of the Health Director or supervisor, the intern will perform administrative work for public health programs and services; gain Municipal Public health experience. May require evening and weekend hours.* ***This is a 6\-week internship program.***

* Assists in the coordination and implementation of public health programs and initiatives
* Supports community outreach activities, including health fairs and vaccination clinics.
* Collects, enters and maintains program data; assists with data analysis and reporting.
* Participates in Community Health Needs Assessments and related surveys.
* Provides support for grant\-funded programs, including documentation and surveys.
* Assists in the development of health education materials and presentations.
* Attend staff meetings and training.
* Collaborate with various divisions (Nursing, Environmental Health \& Homeless Prevention)

***Qualifications and Education Requirements***

Applicants must be a current permanent NJ undergraduate/ graduate student or graduated in less than a year. Must be in progress of receiving or have a degree in Public Health, Health Sciences, Nursing, Social Work, Environmental Health, Health Administration, Urban Planning or related field.

***Preferred Skills***

Entry level knowledge of public health principles and practices.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

Pay: $30\.00 per hour

Work Location: In person
