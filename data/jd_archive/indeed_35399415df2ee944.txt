**DESCRIPTION:**

This 8 week Opto\-Electronics Internship offers a unique opportunity to develop hands\-on skills in a laboratory environment while actively participating in research and development (R\&D) engineering processes. The intern will gain valuable experience in New Product Introduction (NPI), product support, and quality management within the optics and electronics, domain.

The Excelitas Analytical Systems division designs and builds power conversion and flash lighting products to custom requirements in the medical, semiconductor, industrial, security inspection and research equipment markets. Our engineering projects range in scope from minor modifications of existing products to concept\-through\-production of new systems.  


**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**
* Assist in laboratory experiments and data collection using spectrometers and other optical measurement instruments.
* Develop and utilize software tools to process and analyze experimental data.
* Participate in the development of performance metrics and apply design for manufacture (DFM) principles.
* Gain a thorough understanding of lighting performance metrics and the operational principles of Xenon arc light sources.
* Collaborate with engineering teams through regular meetings to report, discuss, and interpret data findings.
* Support the development of intellectual property related to opto\-electronic systems and devices.
* Analyze manufacturing data and engage in troubleshooting and problem\-solving for underperforming products.
* Contribute to quality management initiatives and continuous improvement efforts.

  


**LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES:**
* Hands\-on experience in optics and electronics laboratory techniques.
* Exposure to the full lifecycle of New Product Introduction (NPI) and product support processes.
* Insight into the creation and protection of intellectual property in opto\-electronics.
* Practical understanding of quality management systems and manufacturing data analysis.
* Development of communication and teamwork skills through cross\-functional collaboration.
