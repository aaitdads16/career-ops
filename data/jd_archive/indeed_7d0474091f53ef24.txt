**Your Mission**
----------------


* Assist in building and maintaining dashboards in BI tools (like PowerBI or Looker) to help visualize supply chain performance.

* Currently pursuing or already obtained a degree in Business Analytics, Data Science, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.
* A foundational understanding of SQL and a strong desire to apply it to real\-world data challenges.

**About the Team**
------------------


EMEA Sales Operations delivers actionable insights by partnering with various teams to build sustainable reporting, analytics, and data solutions.


The team's primary focus is on managing the orderbook to create visibility into supply and demand. This supports Account Service and Sales in making timely decisions to release orders to the warehouse and maximize fill rates, all supported by our analytical team.
