 *SAP Internship*

 **Purpose and Objectives**  

We invite you to apply for an exciting internship with the SAP Services Skill Management team. The Skill Management team’s mission is to design and deliver innovative enablement that keeps our 20,000\+ consultants globally at the forefront of technology and customer value. During this internship, you will have an excellent opportunity to develop a wide range of creative, professional and technical skills and be mentored by the best and brightest thought leaders in the training industry.

  

Our team is passionate about learning and development, and you will have a purposeful opportunity to discover:  

* Strategies for creating impactful and compelling visual communications
* Gamification techniques for enhancing learning experiences
* Business intelligence and analytic visualization tools
* Animation design and production using tools such as Powtoon
* Program management for global training events
* Internal organization marketing
* Data analysis
 **Expectations and Tasks**  

Creating compelling digital learning experiences is a difference maker for our consultants. In this internship, you will have a blend of responsibility on the team mainly as a creative design talent but also playing a key role in providing business support to various training initiatives.

 **Your tasks include:**  

* Translating complex concepts into intuitive visualizations
* Producing creative and engaging animated videos to enhance the learning experience
* Designing compelling infographics to communicate information quickly and clearly
* Preparing professional grade presentations for a business and technical audience
* Working extensively on our internal social collaboration platforms (blogs, wikis and forums) while improving the user experience and user engagement through data analysis and innovative design
* Organizing, hosting and supporting our innovation webcasts and technology training events
* Brainstorming solutions to enhance business processes
* Communicating with many different people, in various industries and positions, from around the world
 **Educational Qualifications / Skills and Competencies**
