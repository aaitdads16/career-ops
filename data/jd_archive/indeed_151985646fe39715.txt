Job Description
Annotation Project Management Intern Hiring(Singapore\-Based)


We are the world’s leading video generation startup, building cutting\-edge AI tools that redefine how videos are created.


We’re looking for an Annotation Project Management Intern to support our annotation workflows. You will help manage outsourced annotation tasks, coordinate annotators, track progress, and ensure annotation quality.


No coding experience is required, but you should be organized, detail\-oriented, and interested in AI and video generation.


 Responsibilities

* Manage annotators and support daily annotation operations.
* Participate in internal annotation tasks.
* Write clear annotation guidelines and task instructions.
* Assign tasks and follow up on progress.
* Review annotation results and ensure quality.
* Guide annotators and provide feedback.
* Report platform issues and help improve the workflow.


 Requirements

* Undergraduate, master’s, or visiting student based in Singapore with a valid student pass.
* All majors are welcome; interest in AI, video/image generation, or data annotation is preferred.
* Available for at least 25 hours per week.
* Able to work at least one day per week in our office; other days can be remote.
* Proficient in English communication.
* Detail\-oriented, responsible, and good at coordination.
* Must be willing to sign an NDA before starting.


 What You’ll Gain

* Competitive compensation.
* Hands\-on experience with AI annotation and evaluation workflows.
* Opportunity to work closely with a top\-tier AI team.
* Practical experience in project coordination, quality control, and workflow improvement.
