Company Profile
Founded in 2000, Verztec is a global leader in helping companies expand internationally through our three core service pillars: Digital Learning, Language Services, and Global Creative Design. We believe that quality learning and effective cross\-border communication are vital to enhancing market reach and achieving international growth. Trusted by industry giants like Google, UBS, Siemens, Allianz, and Shangri\-La, Verztec delivers effective learning solutions that elevate organizational performance and facilitate seamless market entry.


Verztec partners with clients to design, develop, and manage online training programs and business communications in over 100 languages. Our suite of services includes custom Learning Management Systems, digitization of in\-house training, and multilingual content versioning to ensure seamless communication and effective knowledge transfer across diverse markets.


In addition to our learning solutions, Verztec offers extensive language services to meet the global needs of businesses. With expertise in over 100 languages, we provide both AI\-driven and professional human translation services, enabling companies to communicate effectively and drive profitable revenue streams as they expand into new territories.


Our dedicated team, consisting of project managers, instructional designers, eLearning engineers, and over 10,000 skilled native translators, delivers innovative learning solutions and professional translation and interpretation services. We help fast\-growing companies and multinational brands break down language barriers and unlock new opportunities on the global stage.
