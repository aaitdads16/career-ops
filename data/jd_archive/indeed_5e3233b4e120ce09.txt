* Review and validate AI\-generated outputs against radiologist standards to assess diagnostic quality and safety.
* Participate in dataset curation, quality control, and protocol development for multimodal imaging data (e.g., chest X\-ray, CT, MRI).


This position may suit you best if you are familiar with the areas and concepts below, and would like to develop your skills within Healthineers:

* Experience in radiology.
