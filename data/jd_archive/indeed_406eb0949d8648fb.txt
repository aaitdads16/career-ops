**Hardware/Software Prototyping Intern (2 to 4 Month Placement)**

**About AcoustoFab**

AcoustoFab is pioneering the next generation of lab automation and precision handling solutions through sound\-based levitation technology. We are a deep\-tech life sciences company building machines that move matter using sound waves. We operate at the intersection of acoustics, high performance computing, and real\-world device engineering.

**Role Overview**

We are seeking a proactive Prototyping Intern for a paid, 2 to 4 month summer placement at our central London lab. This is a high\-impact exploration role where you will help us investigate a potential new product category.

The core of this internship is to answer a specific feasibility question for a new product concept: can we build it, and does it work as expected? You will be responsible for building "low\-fidelity" but functional prototypes that combine web technologies with embedded hardware.

You won't be working in a vacuum \- you will have direct access to our founders, scientists, and engineers for mentorship and technical support across software, electronics, and physics.

**What You’ll Work On**

* Rapid Prototyping: Build functional hardware\-software mock\-ups and prototypes to validate and explore core technical concepts and questions.
* Frontend Web Stack: Develop prototype interfaces and software to control and monitor your hardware prototypes using a web technology stack on the desktop.
* Electronics \& Tinkering: Perform basic circuit assembly, breadboarding, soldering, and debugging of electronic components. Use platforms like Arduino or ESP32 to interface with physical components and sensors.
* Experimental Validation: Plan, design, and run tests on your prototypes, collect data, and communicate the results in order to help the team decide if the product concept is viable.
