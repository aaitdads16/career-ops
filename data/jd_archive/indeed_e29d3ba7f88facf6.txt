**Summary**
-----------


This Registered Nurse position is with the Nursing Service with the Level II Urgent Care located at the Jamaica Plain Campus which provides immediate, walk\-in care for minor injuries and non\-emergency illnesses that are not life\-threatening and need early attention but can include emergency care depending on patient presentation and clinical findings. This is a full\-time position at 40 hours per week.

**Duties**
----------


Execute nursing interventions by collaborating with the inter\-professional team incorporating appropriate standards of care and practice and patient outcomes. Assess and systemically collect any data related to patient health status and work in collaboration with the care team to develop the plan of care. Evaluate patient's response to plan of care and work in collaboration with the inter\-professional team to make any needed adjustments. Utilize data to continuously meet care coordination needs across settings in conjunction with the patient, family and/or other care providers. Assume responsibility and accountability for effectively managing nursing care of individual patients. Document nursing assessment and interventions to reflect comprehensive and integrated approach to patient care. Responsible for patient triage assessment upon entering Urgent Care and priority setting according to Emergency Severity Index (ESI) acuity triage.  

  

Work closely with providers, Urgent Care staff members, and other Jamaica Plain campus staff to expedite labs, patient imaging, radiology exams and management of emergencies, environmental supplies and administrative duties (such as Charge Nurse role) and transfer of patients. Provide medications, cardiac monitoring, IV infusion, respiratory treatments, wound care, fractures and ongoing assessment of patients. Provide patient education and identify discharge planning needs including coordination with social work for after care. Give report to appropriate clinical staff and to paramedics and EMS when transferring patients to a higher level of care.
