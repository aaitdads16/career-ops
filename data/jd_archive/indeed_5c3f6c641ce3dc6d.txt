**Summary**
-----------


The Critical Care Nurse Educator is responsible for assessing, planning, implementing, and evaluating the educational activities and programs for nursing service staff within the VAPAHCS. Works collaboratively and consults with management and multidisciplinary teams to develop, implement, and evaluate educational programs for staff, maintain and enhance clinical competence, and manage ongoing education and competency programs.
