ABOUT THE ROLE
  
  

We are seeking a motivated Transformation Analyst Intern to support cross functional improvement and modernization initiatives across our operations, engineering, supply chain, and finance teams. This internship is ideal for students who enjoy analytical work, structured problem solving, and exposure to real transformation projects inside a manufacturing or operations environment.
  
The intern will assist with data analysis, project coordination, cost modeling, and documentation to help teams execute key initiatives. This role provides hands on experience with ERP systems, Bill of Materials (BOM) analysis, CapEx processes, and project management fundamentals.
  
The analyst plays a critical role in enabling organizational change, improving processes, and supporting capital and operational projects.
