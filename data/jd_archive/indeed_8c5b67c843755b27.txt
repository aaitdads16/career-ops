**Job Description**


We are seeking a motivated and detail\-oriented Summer Intern to support our Compliance and Private Investment teams. This role offers hands\-on experience working with private investment fund data, documents, and regulatory processes within a fast\-paced wealth management environment. Over the course of the internship, you will work closely with team members to review and analyze private fund information, helping ensure data accuracy across internal systems while supporting key compliance and reporting initiatives. You’ll gain exposure to how private funds are structured, governed, and monitored, as well as how regulatory requirements are applied in practice. This is a great opportunity for students interested in finance, legal, or compliance careers to develop analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills while contributing to meaningful, real\-world projects.
