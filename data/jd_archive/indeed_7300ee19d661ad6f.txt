Position title: SAP Digital Sovereignty and Compliance Intern (f/m/d)


Location: Berlin, Germany


Internship duration: 6\-8 weeks


Please address your application to Philipp Töbich.

  

We are seeking two enthusiastic and motivated Digital Sovereignty and Compliance Interns (f/m/d) to support strategic decision\-making with analyses of regulatory obligations and political developments in a dynamic, multi\-national environment.

 **Project scope and deliverables**: In this role, you will

* assist in analyzing regulations and translating them into useful decision\-making input,
* help prepare written reports and presentations on legal developments in relevant markets, and
* support the development of a research repository capturing and organizing applicable laws and regulations.

 **Business impact**: Your work will impact business needs within the SAP Sovereign Services \& Delivery (SS\&D) organization, including by

* contributing to regulatory assessments that support SAP in expanding Sovereign Cloud offerings to meet customer requirements,
* supporting strategic decision\-making: the deliverables produced will feed into internal advisory, operations and customer engagements, and
* expanding team knowledge bases: your research on contemporary developments will contribute to the team’s living data source supporting present operations and future business.

 **Team environment and collaboration**:

* As an SAP Digital Sovereignty and Compliance Intern (f/m/d), you will be part of SAP’s Government Security (GS2\) team, reporting to and receiving guidance from a senior team member. Depending on project needs, you may collaborate with colleagues from additional SAP organizations.
* As we are looking for two interns (f/m/d), you will work closely together with your peer, allowing for a division of tasks, review of outputs, and collaborative synthesis of findings.
* Throughout the internship, you will receive a structured onboarding to familiarize yourself with SAP’s business and relevant internal tools to help you navigate your new role.
