AI Solutions Engineering Intern  



Join Fujitsu’s Technology Strategy Unit as we bring the next generation of products at the intersection of artificial intelligence, infrastructure, and high\-performance computing to market.  

As an AI Solutions Engineering Intern, you’ll work directly with the Product and Research teams to build technical demos, validate real\-world use cases, and help translate cutting\-edge research in AI infrastructure into impactful customer workflows.  

Required:


* Enrolled in a PhD or Master’s program in CS, Physics, Engineering, or a related field
* Hands\-on experience with AI/ML, scientific computing, or cloud\-workflows
* Comfortable building with tools like Jupyter, Docker, Python, and open\-source ML libraries
Strong technical communication and problem\-solving skills  
* 

You’ll be a great fit if…


* You bring deep domain knowledge in one of the following:
* AI in bio/pharma research (e.g. protein modeling, drug discovery)
* AI research (e.g. LLMs, model optimization, training workflows)
* AI in finance (e.g. time series forecasting, risk modeling)
* You’re familiar with (or eager to learn about) GPUs, cloud infrastructure, AI/ML pipelines, or HPC systems
* You thrive in fast\-moving, research\-driven environments and enjoy working across product, research, and engineering
