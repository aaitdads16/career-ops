Are you interested in building a career in digital advertising, SaaS, and customer strategy? Do you want hands\-on exposure to how some of the world’s leading brands use technology to drive marketing performance? Smartly is excited to announce our Summer 2026 Internship Program!



Join our global team of Smartlies and learn from some of the best and brightest in the digital advertising industry. Based in our NYC office, this 10\-week internship program is designed to provide structured learning, hands\-on experience, and meaningful exposure to Customer Success at a fast\-growing technology company.



As a Customer Success Intern, you’ll gain foundational knowledge across the digital advertising ecosystem, Smartly’s platform, enterprise customer strategy, and commercial account management while working alongside cross\-functional teams that support some of the world’s top advertisers.


**As a Customer Success Intern you will...**
--------------------------------------------


* Shadow Customer Success Managers and participate in customer\-facing meetings including onboarding calls, status meetings, QBRs, and renewal discussions
* Learn how Customer Success partners cross\-functionally with Product, Sales, Partnerships, Support, and Marketing teams to drive customer outcomes
* Build foundational knowledge of Smartly’s platform ecosystem and how customers use our products to achieve business goals
* Complete product and digital ecosystem trainings across major advertising platforms including Meta, TikTok, Google, Pinterest, and more
* Support Customer Success workflows through meeting preparation, customer note taking, lifecycle tracking, reporting support, and performance analysis
* Develop an understanding of customer lifecycle management, retention strategy, and growth opportunities
* Contribute to a final project tied to a real business need or customer success initiative
* Collaborate with fellow teams across Smartly’s NYC office

**Throughout the program you'll gain exposure to ...**
------------------------------------------------------


* Digital advertising strategy and performance measurement
* Cross\-functional collaboration in a global SaaS organization
* Commercial fundamentals including customer retention and growth
* Strategic thinking, customer communication, and problem\-solving
* Smartly’s platform, product ecosystem, and industry\-leading advertising technology
