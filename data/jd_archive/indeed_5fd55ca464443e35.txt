HEINEKEN



For over 150 years, we’ve brought people together over a beer. Creating joyful moments and lasting memories. With 500\+ brands in 80\+ countries and 90,000 colleagues worldwide, we’re one of the world’s largest brewers.



We’re proud of our legacy, but even more excited about what’s next. From local pubs to a high tech newly build R\&D; innovations center, we’re shaping the future of beer and beyond. Ready to be part of it? Join our IP, Commerce \& regulatory Affairs team for an exciting internship!



The internship



Join HEINEKEN’s Global Legal Affairs team in Amsterdam and gain hands\-on experience in a dynamic, international environment. As a full member of the team, you will assist with legal research, reviewing and drafting contracts, and advising on marketing campaigns. You will also contribute to global knowledge sharing by publishing updates for our colleagues and responding to ad\-hoc legal queries from around the world.


During your internship you will also get the opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as a company, also outside your department. We give you the trust and freedom, so you can work like a real entrepreneur. We like to bring the outside world inside and therefore we embrace new, fresh ideas with enthusiasm. Of course always with the guidance that is right for you.



The department



The IP, Commerce \& Regulatory Affairs department is part of HEINEKEN Global Legal Affairs, based in the vibrant heart of Amsterdam. We specialize in trademark protection, advertising law, food law and sustainability compliance. We advise on marketing campaigns, high profile global sponsorships, green claims and we are involved in the development of exciting new products and packaging. From advising on the next big product launch to supporting world\-class brand activations, we collaborate closely with our colleagues from Marketing, Research and Development and Corporate Affairs, and our colleagues in Heineken’s operating companies across the globe.
