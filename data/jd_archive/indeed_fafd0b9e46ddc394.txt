About the Team


The Integration Platforms team develops technology to support our ever\-expanding customer fleet from prototype to production. We are at an inflection point — adopting an AI\-first strategy to fundamentally transform how our tools are built, maintained, and scaled. A member of this team will build AI\-powered technology that interfaces with our vehicles and accelerates engineering workflows across the organization.


Examples of products we've shipped and are actively evolving include:

* A tool to interact with any vehicle remotely, collecting status and updating software/configurations
* A tool to analyze trillions of data points for anomalies
* A tool to allow engineers to dynamically query the fleet and collect real\-time responses to questions like "let me see data from a random sample of 100 cars currently driving at least 60 MPH with the windows down and the AC on"
* A technology to live stream data off any vehicle, at anytime, anywhere in the world
* A tool that automatically aggregates information from many sources surrounding an event to build a contextual timeline
* AI agents that automate the software release cycle, feedback triage, and fleet anomaly detection
* MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that make our tools accessible to AI\-powered development workflows
* A centralized AI platform enabling engineers across the organization to contribute to our tools using AI\-assisted development
