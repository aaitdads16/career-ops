Hi future changemaker in Consulting!


Are you passionate about improving customer service and helping organizations rethink how they support their customers? As a Thesis Intern in the Service team at Accenture Song, you’ll explore how organizations can design and improve their customer service operations, processes, and ways of working.

  

In this role, you will combine research and hands\-on consulting experience. You will learn on the job by supporting client engagements, working alongside experienced consultants, and contributing to real transformation projects – while also writing your thesis on a topic that creates real impact.

 **Team** The Service Group focuses on the Customer Experience \& Service Reinventions domain \- the front line of how companies connect with their customers.


Our consultants combine strategy, design, and technology to help global organizations deliver seamless, personalized, and intelligent service experiences.


We are especially exploring how AI can revolutionize customer interactions, streamline service operations, and unlock new business models.

As a thesis intern, your role will consist of two main focus areas:


Research (Thesis)

Consulting Support
