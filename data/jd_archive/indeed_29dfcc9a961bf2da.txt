We are seeking a highly motivated Event Management Trainee / Management Trainee to support the planning and execution of events, while gaining direct exposure to business strategy, partnerships, and operations.


This role offers a unique opportunity to work closely with the CEO, with hands\-on involvement in regional projects across AI, fintech, and tourism industries.

* Assist in planning, coordinating, and executing events (virtual \& physical)
* Support end\-to\-end event operations, including logistics, vendor coordination, and on\-ground execution
* Liaise with partners, clients, and stakeholders across different markets
* Support marketing and communications for events (social media, content, outreach)
* Conduct research and market analysis for new initiatives and partnerships
* Assist CEO in strategic and operational tasks as required
* Prepare reports, presentations, and post\-event evaluations

 **Requirements**

* Currently pursuing or recently graduated in Communications, Events Management, Marketing, or related fields
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills
