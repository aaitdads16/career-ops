The internship



Join our team at HEINEKEN’s Global Talent \& Leadership department, located at our international head office in Amsterdam. As part of the Global Talent Acquisition Centre of Excellence, you’ll support the Global Graduate Program (GGP), our international program designed to attract and develop high\-potential graduates into future leaders of HEINEKEN.



As an intern in the GGP team, you’ll contribute to a range of projects that bring the program to life across the globe. From supporting global recruitment campaigns to coordinating onboarding and community activities, your role will be key in ensuring a consistent and engaging candidate and graduate experience. You’ll also help communicate the right information to the right people, whether it’s future applicants, local HR teams, or the graduates themselves.



You will have the opportunity to work on:


* Supporting the strategic growth of the GGP, focusing on attracting and developing future HEINEKEN leaders.
* Assisting with global recruitment campaign coordination and promotion.
* Managing clear and timely communication for both internal and external audiences.
* Supporting onboarding and guiding graduates during key stages of their journey.
* Helping maintain and improve internal platforms and community tools.
* Coordinating yearly or agile activities for the Global Graduate Community.


Your new team and workplace



You’ll join a small, hands\-on team where ownership and initiative are strongly encouraged, and where your ideas can directly shape the future of the Global Graduate Program.



The Global Graduate Program team sits within Global Talent Acquisition, which is part of Global Talent \& Leadership (GT\&L;), the centre of excellence responsible for unlocking the potential of our people to grow HEINEKEN’s business.



In this internship, you will gain hands\-on experience in how we attract and develop early\-career talent around the world, working closely with a global network of stakeholders across our OpCos.



During your internship, you’ll have every opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as an organization, not just within your own team, but across departments. We trust and empower you to work with an entrepreneurial mindset. At HEINEKEN, we love bringing the outside world in and welcome fresh, new ideas with open arms.
