**Role Summary:**

CPP Investments is seeking an Intern to join the internal audit team, Assurance \& Advisory (“A\&A”), for a four\-month term starting September 2026, based in our Toronto office. The A\&A team provides independent assurance over the adequacy and effectiveness of CPP Investments’ governance, risk management, and internal controls.
  

  

The team performs audits and advisory engagements across the organization, including Public Markets, Private Markets, and Technology \& Operations, with results reported regularly to Management, senior executives, and the Audit Committee. As part of the team, interns contribute directly to audit engagements while gaining an enterprise\-wide perspective across a global investment organization.
  

  

This role supports A\&A’s continued evolution toward more data\-enabled assurance, using technology and analytics to enhance risk identification, testing coverage, and insight generation.
**Accountabilities \& Qualifications:**

* Support the planning and execution of audit engagements, including control testing and business process documentation.
* Aggregate, clean, and analyze data from multiple sources to support audit testing and risk assessment.
* Use programming and analytical techniques to enable full population\-level testing and identify trends, anomalies, or risk indicators.
* Apply data science and automation techniques to optimize risk\-based audit planning, testing, and reporting.
* Evaluate opportunities to deploy Generative AI (GenAI), Copilot, and similar AI\-assisted tools to accelerate insight generation and knowledge management.
* Assist in identifying, collecting, and validating audit evidence and supporting documentation
* Translate analytical findings into clear insights and visualizations to support audit conclusions and management discussions.
* Document work and testing results clearly and accurately in accordance with established audit methodology.
* Collaborate with team members and business partners to understand business context, risks, and data sources.
* Identify opportunities to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of audit testing through automation or process enhancements.
