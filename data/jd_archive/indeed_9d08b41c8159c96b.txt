### **Role Description:**


This industrial placement intern role provides entry\-level, structured experiences within the Global Channels function of CLS Healthcare. The role focuses on supporting day\-to\-day activities, basic analysis, information gathering and documentation that contribute to channel partner evaluation and activation. The Sales \& Product CLS Intern will complete defined tasks that support Sales and Product colleagues, gain exposure to how global channel partnerships operate in a regulated healthcare environment, and develop practical workplace skills aligned to Clinisys’ AI\-enabled ways of working. There may be limited international travel required to support learning and exposure to global teams or partners.

### **Responsibilities:**

* Carry out structured research and data gathering to support channel and referral partner activity.
* Assist with preparing basic summaries, briefing notes and trackers for internal use.
* Support routine administrative and preparatory tasks that contribute to partner evaluation and opportunity reviews.
* Maintain organised records, templates and documentation in line with guidance from the team.
* Capture feedback and information from internal discussions and provided materials, escalating key points to the wider team.
* Assist in organising market, customer or partner information into agreed formats.
* Supports simple comparisons, lists or overviews using guidance and templates provided.
* Help translate scientific or healthcare\-related information into clear notes for internal stakeholders.
* Use approved digital and AI\-enabled tools (e.g. Microsoft Copilot) to support research, note\-taking and documentation.
* Follow established processes, quality standards and data protection requirements, especially in regulated contexts.
