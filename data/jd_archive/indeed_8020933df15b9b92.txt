Internship – Energy \& Water Driving System



“Improving and developing driving system tools to support the sustainability goals of the company gives me lots of energy and lets me enjoy my internship journey”



The internship



HEINEKEN committed to becoming net zero carbon by 2030 (scope 1\&2\) and to reduce its water usage significantly. To reach our goals, we use driving system for utilities (Energy \& Water) usage and aim to enhance this further. During this internship, you will be working on improving the existing driving system template and start the process of simplifying/improving the driving system available.



In this internship you will be responsible for:


* Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities to start the development of future proof versions of the driving system
* Improving the documentation regarding the driving system
* Preparing the 2027 driving system worldwide
* Support development of automated reports and data pipelines
* Interact with other departments (TPM and Business Insight Team) and different regional stakeholders to define the future state of the driving system tools


During your internship you will get the opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as a company, also outside your department. We give you trust and freedom, so you can work like a real entrepreneur. We like to bring the outside world inside and therefore we embrace new, fresh ideas with enthusiasm. Of course, always with the guidance that is right for you.



The department



The Sustainable Production department (part of Global Production, Supply Chain) consists of a multidisciplinary team, working on different subjects of sustainability of our operations such as water and energy usage, decarbonization, wastewater management and reporting. The team is made up of professionals from different countries, expertise and academic background with all one goal to make our operations more sustainable
