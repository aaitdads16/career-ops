#### **About the Position:**



We are seeking a highly motivated intern to support materials development through a rigorous blend of hands\-on characterization and disciplined data workflow design. This role focuses on polymer\-based systems within a wet chemistry environment, with particular emphasis on establishing standardized impedance spectroscopy (EIS) measurement protocols, creating structured experimental datasets, and building the data\-processing foundation needed for future machine\-learning\-guided material optimization. The position is primarily experimental (\~70%) with meaningful exposure to data systems and automation (\~30%).

  


#### **Responsibilities:**


* Prepare, handle, and evaluate samples in a wet chemistry lab environment.
* Establish, document, and standardize impedance spectroscopy (EIS) test methods and measurement conditions for material characterization.
* Conduct repeated EIS measurements with high consistency, reproducibility, and attention to experimental controls.
* Process and analyze experimental data, including impedance and related characterization datasets.
* Extract key material properties and help build a basic feature\-extraction framework that converts raw signals into structured, comparable descriptors.
* Build structured datasets with consistent naming conventions, traceable metadata, and organized records that support downstream analysis.
* Develop robust Python workflows (pandas, numpy, matplotlib, etc.) for automated data cleaning, transformation, structuring, and analysis.
* Build data\-quality and validation checks to identify inconsistencies, missing data, outliers, and measurement issues, and flag them clearly for the broader team.
* Collaborate closely with researchers to improve experimental protocols, strengthen data reliability, and support future machine learning workflows for material optimization.
