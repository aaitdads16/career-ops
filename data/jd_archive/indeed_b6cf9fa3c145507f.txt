**Quantitative research analyst (18\-month contract)**

**Job Description**

The Bridge Project seeks to give every baby in the US a fair start in life. Launched in June 2021 by The Monarch Foundation, The Bridge Project (TBP) is a 501(c)3 organization that supports household stability, maternal health, and healthy infant development through the provision of consistent, unrestricted direct cash to low\-income mothers and children.

The Bridge Project is currently serving more than 3000 babies and their mothers in several states including New York, Wisconsin, Connecticut, West Virgina, Ohio, Kentucky, Massachusetts, New Jersey and Nebraska.

We are on our way to solving child poverty, and we are doing it with dignity, trust, and cash. The Bridge Project has been profiled in The New York Times, CBS, NY1\-Spectrum News, Bloomberg, Live with Kelly and Ryan, and MarketWatch, among others. The Bridge Project was additionally one of Fast Company’s World Changing Ideas of 2022\.

**Job Summary**

The **Quantitative Data Analyst** will be a key member of the research team and will contribute to quantitative and mixed\-method research initiatives across the organization. Under the supervision of the Research Director, the analyst will collaborate closely with Bridge team members and research partners.

The ideal candidate will have demonstrated experience working with quantitative data and a strong interest in applying their skills to advance research in child poverty, maternal health, and child well\-being. The successful candidate will be creative, detail\-oriented, and enjoy working with survey and administrative datasets. The candidate will also demonstrate a strong ability to ensure data accuracy and quality, while adhering to methodological protocols and ethical requirements.
