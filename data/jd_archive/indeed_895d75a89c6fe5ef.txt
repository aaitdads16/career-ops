**Description**  



  

**Your role at GEI.**  

  

GEI’s Bloomfield, NJ office is seeking motivated interns for Summer 2026\. This is an excellent opportunity to learn from our team and gain a wide range of experience in the fields of environmental science, geology, or engineering.  

  

**Essential Responsibilities \& Duties**  

* Assist with analyses and design.
* Perform and observe fieldwork, including observing construction activities.
* Perform computations, prepare spreadsheets, and prepare and edit drawings using AutoCAD.
* Assist with preparing proposals and reports.
* Assist with data management and analysis, and preparation of field documentation and reports.

  

**Minimum Qualifications**  

* Pursuit of a B.S. or M.S. in Environmental Science, Geology, or Environmental, Civil, Geotechnical, Structural, or Waterfront Engineering.
* Minimum 2 years in a technical degree program.
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
* Willingness to travel on assignment, if needed.
* Must have a valid driver's license and be willing to work in the field, as needed.
* Ability to perform field work in active construction sites without restrictions, and in inclement weather, if required.
