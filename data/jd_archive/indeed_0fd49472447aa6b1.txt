### **Job Description**

**Start Date:** July\-September 2026


**Duration:** minimum 6 months (up to 12 months)

**Weekly Presence:** 40h (minimum 32h)


**Location:** Amsterdam (3x per week)


**Internship: Negotiation Excellence**

Experience how Game Theory and Mechanism Design are applied in practice in procurement within Royal Philips, an innovation leader in Healthcare and Consumer Lifestyle. A small team of negotiation experts within Royal PHILIPS Procurement set up to globally support Commodity Managers in high impact negotiations of running business and new awarding. The team's responsibilities include the identification of projects, their preparation and execution. Part of the preparation is to tailor the optimal awarding mechanism design based on Game and Auction Theory.


**Your role:**

* The organization of negotiation trainings and support in the continuous improvement of the training material.
* Support the team during the preparation and execution of high impact negotiations in cooperation with Procurement Commodity Managers and Procurement Engineer.
* Apply AI in daily tasks, but also look for improvements to simulate efficient way of working.

**You're the right fit if:**

* You are currently pursuing a Bachelor or Master degree in in Economics, Business Mathematics, Business Administration, Industrial Engineering, Math or related studies
* Knowledge and enthusiasm for Microeconomics including Game Theory and Mechanism Design
* Independent and organized way of working
* Analytical and abstract thinking
* Excellent knowledge in Excel and Data Analysis
* Fluent in oral and written English.

**In return, we offer you:**

First hand, insights in the entire sourcing process with the experience how to systematically apply theoretical knowledge to business practice. Excellent career prospects, as several of our former interns have transitioned into permanent positions or taken on similar roles in the industry.
