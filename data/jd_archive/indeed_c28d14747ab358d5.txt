**TEAM OVERVIEW:**

Interns will work in the Industrial Engineers, Business Intelligence Analyst, Field Service Engineers, and Technical Training teams. Industrial Engineering consultants lead strategic projects to streamline business processes, improve supply chain and operations, reduce costs, and improve business performance. The team is responsible project life cycle to start with analysis of the as\-is process, identify and quantify opportunities, develop improvement streams, secure buy\-in from stakeholders, and drive implementation.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Structure and break down complex business problems, develop hypothesis, drive analysis and deliver recommendations in a data\-driven manner
* Lead thoughtful and rigorous analysis across large data sets and synthesize insights and opportunities
* Develop and present high\-quality recommendations and deliverables for executive level consumption and contribute to associated strategy and execution discussions
* Work collaboratively across a cross\-functional set of stakeholders to gather the right information, pressure test your hypothesis, co\-create deliverables, accelerate execution and unlock value
* Optimize operations and business processes to improve performance, reduce cycle time, and shrink costs
