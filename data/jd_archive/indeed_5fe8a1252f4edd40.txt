Internship – Talent Acquisition Data Analyst



‘’In this internship, you will gain experience working with Talent Acquisition and People Analytics teams and have the freedom to contribute ideas which shape how we use data across our recruitment efforts.’’



The internship



HEINEKEN is on a journey to become the best\-connected brewer in the world. This ambition is supported by our Talent Acquisition Team in bringing in the best Talents for our organization. One of our focus areas is using more data and insights in our recruitment efforts but also sharing data with the business. So, we are looking for ways we can ensure we have Talent Acquisition data at our fingertips.



In this internship you will be responsible for the following:


* Work directly with TA Manager, TA Leads and Talent Acquisition Business Partners, and People Operations to gather requirements, provide status updates, and build relationships.
* Interacting with our regional and global people function colleagues to understand and capture their feedback and requirements. Our business is dynamic so new queries arise regularly. New requirements must be evaluated in terms of the value they will deliver, and the complexity of the changes required before they are prioritized and implemented.
* Support on progressing our Talent Acquisition and People Analytics use cases.
* Work with multiple datasets to extract insights.
* Evaluate organizational methods and provide source\-to\-target mappings and information\-model specification documents for datasets


Your new team and workplace



The Talent Acquisition Team interacts with colleagues from different Operating Companies based in the Netherlands. Next to that, there is also a project side with the Global Supply Team. You will work together with these colleagues on a day\-to\-day basis. We also interact with different People disciplines in Amsterdam, such as the People Analytics and Global Talent Acquisition. Our team likes to socialize and enjoy each other’s company. There will be plenty of team moments.



During your internship, you’ll have every opportunity to get to know HEINEKEN as an organization, not just within your own team, but across departments. We trust and empower you to work with an entrepreneurial mindset. At HEINEKEN, we love bringing the outside world in and welcoming fresh, new ideas with open arms. Of course, we’ll make sure you get the guidance that suits you.



The unique you!


We believe everyone has unique talents, which is why we’re looking for you! Energy, drive and the ambition to grow are what make the difference at HEINEKEN. In addition, you bring:


* Strong communication skills in English, both written and verbal
* Analytical mindset, strong numeracy skills and attention to detail.
* Willingness to learn different technologies and adapt to changing requirements.


Even if you feel you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply. An internship is the perfect opportunity to learn and grow!
