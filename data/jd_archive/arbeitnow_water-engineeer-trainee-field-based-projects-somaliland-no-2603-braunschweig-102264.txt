<p><strong>CES is looking for a Water Engineer Trainee – Field-Based Projects (Somaliland), Contract (18 months)</strong></p>
<p>CES is seeking a motivated graduate to join our team as a Water Engineer Trainee, supporting the implementation of our water infrastructure project in Somaliland. The Project is designed to improve land, water and soil management in selected rural communities with the purpose to use soil and water resources in a more sustainable and climate resilient way. Project intervention measures comprise water harvesting structures, soil and water conservation measures, and comprehensive accompanying measures.</p>
<p>This is a paid trainee position offering direct involvement in real engineering projects, including dams, water harvesting systems, and hydraulic structures. The role combines office-based work in Germany with extended field assignments in Somaliland. You will work closely with experienced engineers and gain hands-on experience in both technical and field-based project work. CES will provide appropriate security arrangements and support for all field missions.</p>
<p><strong>Your Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p>You will support our project teams in the preparation and implementation of water infrastructure projects, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Supporting hydraulic and hydrological analyses</li>
<li>Assisting in the planning and design of dams and water retention structures</li>
<li>Supporting site investigations and field assessments</li>
<li>Participating in construction supervision and technical reporting</li>
<li>Supporting coordination with local partners and stakeholders</li>
</ul>
