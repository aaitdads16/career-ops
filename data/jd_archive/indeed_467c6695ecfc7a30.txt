### **The Role**



We are looking for a high\-energy, driven, and articulate Training Intern to join us during a pivotal time. Under the direction of our Senior Manager of Product Training, you will start part\-time (approximately 15–20 hours/week) to learn the ropes of the Hyperproof platform and our training methodology. By summer, you will transition to full\-time to provide primary coverage for our Training Manager's leave.



This isn't a "shadowing" internship. You will be in the driver's seat, facilitating live sessions and managing our Learning Management System (LMS). This is a temporary position which should conclude by the end of summer.


### **What You'll Do**


* **Facilitate \& Present:** Facilitate live, camera\-on training sessions via Zoom for new and existing customers, partnering closely with product management to present new features in Hyperproof.
* **Content Management:** Creating training video content by partnering with marketing to deliver product "level\-up" early\-access training videos and update existing training content, text, screenshots, and videos. Leverage AI voiceover tools and advanced video editing.
* **Operational Support:** Manage the training calendar, coordinate session logistics, and handle administrative tasks within our learning management system Bridge.
* **Learn \& Adapt:** Learn the Hyperproof GRC software , LMS, and training content to serve as a resource for end users on questions. .
* **Measure \& Reward Learner Success:** Distribute community badges within the customer community and analyze performance metrics via surveys to report in the support and enablement department.
* **Drive Product Awareness:** Partner with the documentation team to translate bi\-weekly release notes into high\-impact customer\-facing release communications, leveraging our Intercom AI chatbot.
* **Independent Ownership:** Work autonomously in a remote environment, taking initiative to solve problems and improve the training experience.

### **Who You Are**


* **On\-Camera Pro:** You have an outgoing personality and feel energized by public speaking or facilitating groups.
* **Tech\-Fluent (Not Tech\-Heavy):** You don't need to be a developer, but you should be a "power user" of Google Suite and Zoom. Prior exposure to an LMS (e.g., Bridge, Docebo, TalentLMS, Seismic) is a huge plus.
* **AI Integration \& Synthesis:** I am an early adopter of generative AI, utilizing AI chat tools and voiceover synthesis to rapidly produce and scale high\-quality, customer\-facing training materials.
* **Multimedia Savvy:** You aren't just a facilitator; you're a builder. You bring hands\-on experience with video editing (ideally Camtasia) and are excited to use AI voiceover and editing tools to scale our customer\-facing library.
* **Driven \& Independent:** You thrive in remote settings and don't need someone looking over your shoulder to get things done.
* **High EQ:** You can read a virtual room and adjust your communication style
