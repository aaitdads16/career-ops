**Job Description**
-------------------


The Department of Psychology invites applications for a Temporary Management Support Officer (6\-month contract) to support operations at the Clinical and Healthy Psychology Centre (CHPC).

  

The new staff will support CHPC’s operations in the following areas (including but not limited to):


* Attending to email, phone, and WhatsApp queries related to Professional Services
* Managing online and referral registrations for CHPC clients
* Scheduling Professional Services appointments between clients and faculty staff
* Following up promptly on Professional Service payments from clients
* Following up with faculty for Professional Service session notes
* Receiving clients for Professional Service appointments at CHPC and locking up the clinic at day’s end
* Supporting intern observation scheduling for Professional Service clients, where applicable
* Assisting faculty with availability checks, room bookings, and pre session preparation
* Preparing for the system migration for appointment scheduling, including verifying that OneDrive client folders are updated with required documents, notes, and case file numbers
