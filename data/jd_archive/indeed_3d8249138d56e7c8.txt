KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
  
  

Data Extraction \& Management
  
* Extract, consolidate, and validate data from multiple systems across the manufacturing environment (SAP, MES, manual logs, and other sources)
* Ensure data integrity, consistency, and reliability for operational decision\-making
* Identify gaps in data collection and partner with teams to improve data capture processes
* Support development of standardized data structures for reporting and analytics


Dashboard Development \& Visualization
  
* Design, build, and maintain dashboards to track key performance indicators, including:
* Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
* Throughput and production output
* Scrap and yield performance
* Downtime and equipment utilization
* Create clear, actionable visualizations using Power BI, Tableau, or Excel
* Continuously improve dashboards to support real\-time decision making on the production floor


Manufacturing Performance \& Analytics
  
* Analyze production, quality, and maintenance data to identify trends and inefficiencies
* Support root cause analysis efforts with data tied to downtime, scrap, and performance losses
* Partner with Operations and Engineering to quantify performance gaps and track improvements
* Assist in building leading indicators or basic predictive models for equipment performance


Cross\-Functional Collaboration
  
* Work directly with Operations, Manufacturing Engineering, Maintenance, and Supply Chain teams
* Translate operational challenges into data\-driven insights and recommendations
* Support alignment between data, planning, and execution
* Participate in daily and weekly operational reviews to provide visibility into performance


Continuous Improvement \& Strategy Support
  
* Support development of data\-driven strategies to improve plant performance
* Assist initiatives focused on improving:
* Equipment uptime
* Labor efficiency
* Production flow
* Cost per unit (CPU)
* Identify opportunities to automate reporting and reduce manual processes


Communication \& Reporting
  
* Present findings, trends, and recommendations in a clear and concise way
* Build reports that are easy to interpret and drive action
* Support leadership with data insights for operational and strategic decisions
