About the Team


The Firmware Content Management team ensures that in\-car content and features are localized to users around the world. We’re also responsible for the development and integration of software Release Notes — the first thing customers see when they receive an update.


As an intern, you’ll sit at the intersection of content, code, and global impact, helping build tools and automation that allow us to deliver high\-quality, localized updates faster and more efficiently. You’ll take ownership of the end\-to\-end integration of Release Notes for Tesla’s over\-the\-air (OTA) software updates, ensuring every customer receives the right content for their vehicle. Your work will have immediate, visible impact — including on social media, where Release Notes are frequently shared by users around the world.

 **What You'll Do*** Collaborate with developers and feature owners to support accurate and timely Release Notes integration
* Build web services to streamline content management and deployment
* Create automation scripts to simplify localization and content workflows
* Test and validate content to ensure seamless delivery in software updates
* Develop tools and reporting frameworks to monitor integration health and automation reliability
