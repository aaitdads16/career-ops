**Software Engineering Intern**

**What You’ll Do (Your Learning Opportunities)**

As a **Software Engineering Intern** , you will gain real\-world exposure to IT systems and support work. Your responsibilities will include:


* Working on incident tickets by updating status and tracking progress using a Service Management tool
* Learning how to analyze and troubleshoot basic technical or system\-related issues
* Assisting with simple system enhancements and report requests
* Supporting testing activities for system changes, including:



	+ Creating basic test cases
	+ Preparing test data
	+ Checking and documenting test results
* Helping to prepare step\-by\-step instructions for deploying changes into the production environment
* Compiling weekly or monthly summaries of incident tickets and service requests
* Supporting other tasks or projects assigned by the team or account manager

* Undergraduates currently enrolled in a Diploma or Degree program at a college or university
