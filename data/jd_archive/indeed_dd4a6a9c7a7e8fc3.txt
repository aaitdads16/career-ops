**OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENT:**
The Onboard Fare Systems team provides and supports ticket selling services for both the MNR and LIRR Transportation Departments. These services include both the Onboard Ticket Issuing Machines as well as back\-end support to allow for ticket selling and reporting capabilities.

The fellow will need to learn the basics of how credit and debit card information must be protected. Your main task will be to get familiar with how the current Onboard Ticket Issuing Machine works, including the devices connected to it (like card readers, printers, or scanners). After that, you will research other hardware options that could replace or improve the current setup. These options should include devices that can safely accept credit and debit card payments and meet PCI security requirements. Familiarity with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI) or Mobile Point of Sale is preferred.  

At the end of the project, you will create a report that compares the different hardware solutions you found. This report will include:  

* A simple comparison chart or matrix
* A list of pros and cons for each option
* A clear summary of which solution you recommend and why
