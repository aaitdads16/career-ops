We are looking for a **Summer Intern** to join our **Global Growth Solutions** team on a **3\-month fixed\-term contract**. This is an excellent opportunity for a student, recent graduate, or early\-career candidate to gain practical experience in data analysis, market research, and project support within the investment management industry.


In this role, you will support day\-to\-day analytical activities and contribute to a defined project. You will work with data, help identify trends and insights, and assist in preparing reports, presentations, and project materials. Working alongside experienced colleagues, you will gain exposure to how data, market intelligence, and strategic thinking help asset managers and financial institutions make informed business decisions.

**Responsibilities:**
---------------------

* Contribute to a defined project during the internship
* Support research, analysis, and reporting activities across the team
* Assist with data collection, validation, management, and interpretation
* Analyse data to identify trends, themes, and commercial insights across assets, flows, revenues, and market activity
* Use Excel, PowerPoint, and other analytical tools to interpret data and communicate findings clearly and effectively
* Support the preparation of presentations, reports, and project materials for internal and client\-related use
* Work with colleagues across the team to support project delivery and broader team priorities

**Qualifications:**
-------------------

* Studying for, or recently completed, a degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field
* Understanding of asset management, financial markets, and data\-led decision\-making
* Strong technical skills, including Excel and PowerPoint, with knowledge of tools such as Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, or Power Apps
* Tech\-savvy and comfortable working with data, digital tools, and research
* Strong analytical, numerical, and problem\-solving skills
* Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present insights effectively
* Organised, detail\-oriented, curious, proactive, and eager to learn in a team environment
