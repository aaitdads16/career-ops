Company Profile
ECICS Limited is a Singapore\-based general insurer with more than 50 years of experience providing insurance solutions for individuals and businesses. Established in 1975, the company offers a wide range of products including motor, maid, home, personal accident, and commercial insurance, and is regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). As a homegrown insurer and subsidiary of IFS Capital Limited, ECICS is known for its customer\-focused approach, reliable coverage, and strong local expertise.


Job Description
We are looking for a motivated and analytical Underwriting Transformation Intern to support the development and enhancement of automated underwriting systems. This internship offers hands\-on exposure to rule\-based decision engines, API integrations, process optimization, and data workflows.


You will work closely with underwriting, product, and technology teams to help streamline underwriting operations and improve automation performance.

 **Key Responsibilities**

* Assist in building and improving automated underwriting workflows
* Support development of rule\-based and logic\-driven decision systems
* Work on API integrations between underwriting systems and third\-party data sources
* Analyse underwriting process gaps and identify automation opportunities
* Help document business logic and translate underwriting requirements into technical specifications
* Build dashboards and monitoring tools to track automation performance
* Participate in testing, validation, and deployment of automation tools
* Support data cleaning, transformation, and pipeline optimization

 **Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Finance, or a related field
* Basic understanding of logical decision\-making or rule\-based systems
* Familiarity with SQL and/or Python (academic or project experience acceptable)
* Understanding of APIs and data structures (preferred but not mandatory)
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving mindset
* Good documentation and communication skills
* Attention to detail and eagerness to learn

 **Learning Opportunities**

* Exposure to real\-world underwriting and risk assessment frameworks
* Hands\-on experience with workflow automation and decision engines
* Practical understanding of API integrations and system architecture
* Experience working with structured and semi\-structured data
* Cross\-functional collaboration with risk, tech, and product teams
