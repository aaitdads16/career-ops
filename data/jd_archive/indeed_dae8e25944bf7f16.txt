Job Description
The Lighting Design Intern will support the design team in developing lighting concepts, preparing design materials, and assisting with project coordination for assigned projects. The intern will gain practical exposure to lighting design workflows, product applications, technical considerations, and project execution processes within the lighting and interior design industry.

**Requirements:**

* Currently pursuing a Diploma or Degree in Interior Design, Architecture, Lighting Design, or a related discipline.
* Interest in architectural/interior lighting and willingness to learn lighting products and applications.
* Basic proficiency in AutoCAD, SketchUp, or related design/documentation software will be an advantage.
* Organized, detail\-oriented, and able to maintain proper documentation and file management for drawings, revisions and documents.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
* Able to work independently, manage timelines, and adapt to changing project requirements.
* Positive attitude, willingness to learn, and eagerness to gain industry exposure.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1\. Design \& Project Support**

* Assist in developing basic lighting design concepts and proposals for assigned projects.
* Support the preparation of lighting layouts, drawings, mood boards, and presentation materials.
* Assist in updating and revising drawings and design proposals based on feedback from senior designers, clients, and internal teams.
* Support the preparation of design documentation for client presentations and internal reviews.
