About NEXFT


NEXFT is an early\-stage Singapore\-based AI startup building "digital employee" solutions across three areas: AI HR, AI Customer Service, and AI Compliance. Our clients are in finance, manufacturing, data centers, and logistics — industries with heavy manual workflows and repetitive information processing, where AI can meaningfully reduce workload and human error.


We have active paying clients, real deployments in production, and support from Singapore government innovation programs. Small team, fast pace, real impact.


The Role


You'll work directly with the founders to make sure our AI products actually work in real client environments — not just in demos. Expect to test a new AI HR workflow in the morning, help deploy a compliance bot at a client site in the afternoon, and write up bugs before the day ends.


This is a hands\-on role for someone who likes seeing AI products go from "it works on my laptop" to "it's running in a bank's back office."


What You'll Do


QA \& Testing — Test AI workflows, web apps, and automation tools across our HR, CS, and compliance products. Find bugs, edge cases, and usability issues before clients do.


Deployment Support — Help onboard new clients: software setup, integrations, and occasional hardware (tablets, kiosks, on\-prem servers for finance / data center clients with strict data requirements).


Security \& Reliability Checks — Basic vulnerability awareness and data handling reviews — important because our clients are in regulated industries.


Client Troubleshooting — Support clients during onboarding and early usage, in English and (ideally) Mandarin.
