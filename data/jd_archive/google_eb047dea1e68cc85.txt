Role Description  

This is a remote internship role for a Data Analytics Internship & Training Program. The role will involve day-to-day tasks related to data analytics, including data management, data analysis, data modelling, and developing analytical skills. The intern will have the opportunity to work on real-world data projects and gain practical experience in the field of data analytics. 




Responsibilities  

Work closely with our esteemed data science team to gather, refine, and analyse extensive datasets, refining skills in data pre-processing and visualization. Apply machine learning and data mining methods to derive insights from large datasets, including identifying Excel interface structures. Utilize Python programming for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in practical business contexts, with a focus on risk analytics in banking and finance. Acquire and apply advanced analytical techniques using tools like Tableau. Create reports and presentations to effectively convey data analysis methodologies and findings.  


Qualifications  

Strong problem-solving and critical thinking abilities Currently pursuing any degree showcasing a strong commitment to continuous learning and professional growth. Excellent communication and teamwork skills Ability to work independently and remotely
