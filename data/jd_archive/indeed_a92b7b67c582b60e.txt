**Duties**
----------


Total Rewards of a Allied Health Professional  

  

The Health Technician (Telehealth Clinical) \- Advanced, T38 Hybrid, GS\-0640\-07 provides high\-level support and management of telehealth operations at clinical care sites, potentially requiring travel. Responsibilities include delivering telehealth competency\-based training, supporting clinical telehealth encounters, and managing real\-time telehealth events. The incumbent coordinates, installs, maintains telehealth technologies, and evaluates telehealth operations for performance improvement. They also provide technical support, schedule telehealth appointments, and handle various program support duties. Acting independently, the position involves preparing medical equipment, serving as a telepresenter, managing clinical data for telehealth applications, and ensuring patient privacy and equipment maintenance. Additionally, the Health Technician participates in performance improvement activities, provides patient education, and addresses technical and administrative issues related to telehealth services, requiring minimal supervision and occasionally guiding less experienced staff and other duties as assigned.  

  

**Major duties include, but are not limited to the following:**


* Deliver established telehealth competency based training for Clinicians and telehealth staff members.
* Supports Clinical telehealth encounters for both the patient and provider locations by serving as the tele\-presenter within scope of practice and functioning as an imager.
* Support clinical data for telehealth store and forward applications.
* Manage real time clinical telehealth events, providing established patient education and providing technical and administrative support of telehealth operations.
* Schedule simple and complex intra\-facility and inter\-facility telehealth appointments.
* Install, configure, maintain and troubleshoot technology and perform a variety of program support duties as assigned.
* Travel to cross cover sister sites within Nor Cal.
* Other duties as assigned.
