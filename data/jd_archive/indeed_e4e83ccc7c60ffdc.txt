**Overview:**


Draper is an independent, nonprofit research and development company headquartered in Cambridge, MA. The 2,000\+ employees of Draper tackle important national challenges with a promise of delivering successful and usable solutions. From military defense and space exploration to biomedical engineering, lives often depend on the solutions we provide. Our multidisciplinary teams of engineers and scientists work in a collaborative environment that inspires the cross\-fertilization of ideas necessary for true innovation. For more information about Draper, visit www.draper.com.

**Job Description Summary:**


This position is for students looking for engineering opportunities for Summer 2026\.  

  

As a student in Production Planning, you will be exposed to a broad range of technical challenges where you can apply your knowledge as well as learn new skills while seeing how problems of national interest are solved. Your experience can range from applying engineering principles and best practices to lead process improvements to designing layouts and completing time studies on the manufacturing floor.
  

  

While working under guidance, you will lead data\-driven decision\-making and new production initiatives. You will work directly with engineering, operations, and leadership teams to optimize manufacturing processes, improve efficiency, and support the launch of new production lines. This is a great opportunity for someone energetic and process\-driven who wants real plant exposure, ownership, and strong career growth. You will also have the opportunity to interact with other teams such as materials management or metrology.  

  

Assignments are designed to develop a practical knowledge of processes, procedures, facilities, and systems. Assignments will also provide an opportunity to develop valuable networking connections which will help prepare and develop your skills for future career opportunities. Actual project tasks will depend on immediate program priorities.  

  

Job Description  

Duties/Responsibilities  

* Document scheduling procedures, update standard operating procedures (SOPs), and support schedule reporting for leadership.
* Support continuous improvement efforts to streamline scheduling workflows, planning tools, and communication processes
* Assist in building, updating, and maintaining daily, weekly, and monthly production schedules based on manufacturing capacity, material availability, and demand priorities.
* Analyze production data, historical performance, and capacity constraints to support schedule accuracy and feasibility.
* Conduct time studies and data analysis to uncover bottlenecks.
* Collaborate with supply chain to optimize material flow.
* Analyze and improve business processes to reduce waste and improve efficiency
* Process development
* Continuous improvement
* Other duties as required
