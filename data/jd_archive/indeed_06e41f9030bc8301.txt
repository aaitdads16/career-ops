**AI Engineering Summer Intern Stamford, CT****Position Overview**
Information Services Group (ISG) is seeking a small group of highly capable technical interns to join our AI Acceleration Unit for an intensive summer build program focused on applied enterprise AI.
This is not a research internship. Interns will work directly with ISG leadership and engineers to design and build production\-grade AI agents, automation tools, and analytics capabilities that support consulting delivery, benchmarking, financial modeling, and internal productivity.
The program culminates in a formal executive demonstration of the solutions developed during the program. It is designed for students who want to ship real AI products, not just experiment with models.**Responsibilities**
Interns will collaborate with ISG’s AI team to build real enterprise solutions, such as:

* AI agents that automate consulting workflows
* LLM\-powered research and benchmarking assistants
* Data engineering pipelines supporting analytics and financial models
* Automation tools that enhance internal productivity and delivery operations
* Structured and unstructured data analysis using modern AI frameworks


All projects will have defined problem statements, executive sponsors, and clear build objectives.
