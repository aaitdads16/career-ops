### **What You’ll Be Doing To Make Organizations Data\-Driven**

You'll join the **Delivery practice**, working directly with our Head of Delivery and our Delivery Leads. The role splits roughly **50/50** between hands\-on delivery support and a research project of your own.

 **Half your time: supporting the delivery practice.** You'll work shoulder\-to\-shoulder with the Delivery Leads to keep our engagements running smoothly. That means:  

  

Every engagement we run has a paper trail behind it — and you'll be the one shaping a lot of it. **Kickoff decks** that set the tone with a new client, **status reports** that keep stakeholders honest, **closeout materials** that capture what we built and what we learned. You'll take rough inputs from a Delivery Lead and turn them into something sharp enough to land in a client's inbox.  

Alongside that, you'll handle **delivery\-side finance tasks**: tracking spend against budget, supporting invoicing cycles, and flagging variances early so they can be addressed before they become problems. You don't need to be a finance whiz on day one, but you'll leave knowing how a consultancy actually makes (and loses) money on a project.  

  

You'll also be the connective tissue between **sales and our consultants** — coordinating when new engagements come in, making sure the handover from sale to delivery happens cleanly, and chasing down the small details that otherwise fall through the cracks.
