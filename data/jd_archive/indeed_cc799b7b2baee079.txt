**Job Description**

We are offering a paid 8\-week Summer Internship for Oxford Brookes law students interested in the future of legal research and legal technology.


This internship provides hands\-on experience working across our Legal Content and Product teams, offering exposure to how AI is reshaping the way lawyers and law students research, analyse and apply the law.


Interns will contribute to meaningful projects, gain insight into commercial legal technology environments, and work collaboratively to complete a structured project exploring the future of AI in legal research.


This is a fixed\-term internship based in the UK, with a hybrid working model including London office days and some days working from home.


**Responsibilities**

During the internship, you will:


* Work across LexisNexis’ teams including Content, Product, UX and Sales
* Explore how AI is reshaping legal research and content development
* Contribute to real product and content discussions
* Support research and analysis relating to legal study and AI\-enabled tools
* Contribute to a structured project on the future of legal research
