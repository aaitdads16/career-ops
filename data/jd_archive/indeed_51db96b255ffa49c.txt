**Role Overview**



The RTW ADF Summer Intern will work closely with the NewCo\-Corporate Development team at RTW to research, plan, and implement firm strategic initiatives as well as contribute to New Ventures workstreams.



This role will support high\-priority, high\-impact projects. In partnership with cross\-functional stakeholders, this individual will drive company operations and forward\-thinking organizational goals. The successful candidate will have strong analytical skills, capability to do secondary research, be deeply collaborative, an outstanding communicator, and thrive in a fast\-paced, entrepreneurial environment.



This position is full\-time and reports to an **Associate on the ADF team.**

This person will play an integral role in company building, business development, alliance management, corporate strategy, and financing efforts to help grow and scale RTW new ventures. * Provide support to RTW’s portfolio companies in managing partnerships, sourcing vendors, and assisting with business development initiatives

* Contribute to various projects related to company building, financial and scientific analysis, project management, and other ad hoc initiatives as needed
