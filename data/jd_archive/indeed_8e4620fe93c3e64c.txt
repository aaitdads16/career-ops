Our fast\-paced environmental consulting firm is seeking motivated interns to support our team of Wetland Scientists during the summer field season. This internship is an excellent opportunity for students or recent graduates interested in environmental science, ecology, wetlands, wildlife biology, conservation, GIS, or ecological consulting.

Interns will work alongside experienced environmental professionals and gain hands\-on exposure to a wide variety of environmental consulting projects throughout Massachusetts. This is a great opportunity to learn how environmental projects move from field investigation through permitting and regulatory review.

This position is designed to support Wetland Scientists in all aspects of their work while providing valuable professional experience and mentorship in the environmental consulting field.

**What You’ll Gain Exposure To**

* Wetland delineations
* Environmental site inspections
* Wetland permitting processes
* Conservation Commission hearings
* Wildlife and habitat observations
* GPS and field data collection
* Environmental compliance monitoring
* Report preparation and environmental documentation
* Site plan and map review
* Client and contractor coordination
* Real\-world ecological consulting projects

**Responsibilities**

Interns will assist Wetland Scientists with some or all of the following:

* Field preparation and equipment organization
* Collecting and documenting field data
* Assisting with environmental site visits and inspections
* Preparing maps, photographs, and project files
* Organizing field notes and project documentation
* Supporting report preparation and administrative project tasks
* Traveling to field sites throughout Massachusetts
* Assisting with general project coordination and team support
